## In-range UV and pixel-impact bound

- The original sprite corner UVs are `(0,0)` through `(1,1)` (`Game/src/GameObjects/Sprite.cpp:19-22`), with X expansion only for repeated sprites (`:215-234`). The private quad likewise interpolates endpoint U/V values 0/1, with horizontal flips and PNG-row V inversion (`port/linux/SDLRenderer.cpp:324-335`). Thus an out-of-range UV is **not required** for address mode to matter. Under linear filtering at mip width `W`, the sample footprint crosses a horizontal boundary for in-range `0 <= u < 1/(2W)` or `1-1/(2W) < u <= 1`; the analogous vertical band uses mip height `H`. Flips swap which screen edge sees each band.
- I decoded the tracked `Game/Media/objects/raft.png` read-only with Pillow: 1024×280 RGBA, SHA-256 `d319f5d1cc292565df20111a67529feb8b4ff3434ff2176fa3d7ddf5f420d031`. Its nonzero-alpha bounding box is `(5,12,1012,265)` (right/bottom exclusive). The entire left/right/top/bottom outermost rows/columns have alpha 0. Leftmost RGB is black while rightmost RGB is white, both with alpha 0 (corner samples `(0,0,0,0)` and `(255,255,255,0)`). Therefore, **at base mip**, e.g. an in-range `u=1/4096`, bilinear `REPEAT` mixes the opposite-edge RGB into the returned raw sample while `CLAMP_TO_EDGE` does not. At that boundary footprint both sampled texel alphas are zero, so this base-level raw-RGB difference contributes no color under the private draw's `SRC_ALPHA, ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA` blend (`SDLRenderer.cpp:310-316`). The top/bottom base rows have equal RGBA at corresponding x coordinates, so a V-only cross-boundary base-level sample has no demonstrated difference there.
- The source nominally projects each 458.298584×125.316017 raft to ~305.5324×83.5440 logical pixels (`port/game/LevelScene.cpp:1195,1269-1271`), ~3.3515 source texels/output pixel on either axis. With the branch's `GL_LINEAR_MIPMAP_LINEAR` (`SDLRenderer.cpp:875-880`) this suggests a fractional LOD near 1.75, **not a measured live LOD**. At a conventional local 2×2 downsample, levels 1 and 2 keep their outermost alpha texels at zero because the original transparent margin is at least 5 source texels left, 12 right/top, 15 bottom. That makes a visible wrap/clamp difference at the nominal level-1/2 seam unlikely, but this CPU observation is **not a guarantee** about the implementation-defined `glGenerateMipmap` contents, actual fragment-center UVs, drawable viewport/LOD, overlap, or the final framebuffer. Larger selected mips could mix nonzero alpha into their edge texels. No actual ID11/57/58 fragment hit, mip content, address-mode A/B, or changed composited pixel was observed.
