## Source finding and change

The 1280×720@60 prepatch Forest route previously produced a 1280×688 BMP; that artifact was correctly rejected. The external initiator of that geometry change remains unknown. Fullscreen initialization checks the advertised/active mode and renderer output, but ordinary `SDLRenderer::SaveFrame` used the **current** output size without checking it against the requested fullscreen surface. Thus a later external resize/mode change could silently create a wrong-sized BMP and contaminate exact-pixel evidence. `PresentationSurfaceStable` guarded opt-in refresh-only replay, not ordinary capture. `Mouse()` maps SDL window coordinates through current renderer-output `LogicalViewport` into authored 1280×720 coordinates; an unexpected output can be mapped consistently without making the capture valid.

The minimal production fix is fail-closed capture validation: before and after output readback, verify fullscreen flag/not minimized, requested window and renderer-output dimensions, active display dimensions and requested refresh (if specified). Do not require focus merely to capture a diagnostic image. Refresh-slot focus policy remains unchanged. Scene-target 1280×720 diagnostic captures retain their existing behavior. On mismatch `SaveFrame` returns a descriptive error and the main route exits rather than writing a misleading output BMP. This does **not** restore a drifting mode, identify its external initiator, or guarantee a mode cannot change during BMP file writing.

`DisplaySurfaceGeometry` is a small pure contract used by the runtime. Its CPU fixture rejects the observed 1280×688 output, independent window mismatch, mode/refresh mismatch, windowed/minimized states, and accepts the two tested advertised geometries. Existing viewport fixture plus the new fixture check input-center mapping at 1280×720, 1440×810 and 1280×688 (odd-width letterbox rounding allowed). These are CPU checks, not actual physical mouse events.
