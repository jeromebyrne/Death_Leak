# Next active-gameplay video fidelity contract — forest small orbs (2026-10-22)

## Decision

The highest-impact **still-unfixed, source-proven forest/waterfall visual composition gap for the existing forest-1 gameplay-video route** is the forest's authored small `CurrencyOrb` motion **and its attached tracking trail as one event**. The current port animates their texture but classifies only *waterfall-top* orbs into its hover/tracking path; forest orbs therefore remain at their XML centers and never trigger the source's bright `orb_shatter.png` trail. This affects 22 repeated focal accents, including orbs near the player in the active traversal, rather than a single edge or one transient hit. This is a code-contract and recurrence ranking, **not** a measured Windows/Linux visual delta. It also does not explain the broad lower-foreground/shaft appearance in unmatched Steam stills.

The detailed mechanics and patch seams are already documented in `FOREST_ORB_TRAIL_SCOPE_2026-10-22.md`, `NEXT_FOREST_EFFECT_CONTRACT_2026-10-22.md`, and `ORB_TRAIL_PATCH_PLAN_2026-10-22.md`; this report specifies the **next video discriminator and acceptance boundary**, not a second implementation design. At inspected HEAD `bbc113b`, targeted inspection of `LevelScene.cpp` still shows the waterfall-only predicate and no `orb_shatter` reference; do not assume another worker's planned patch has landed.
