## Source map of the measured expensive draw

The warm-frame `glDrawArrays` calls 7473 and 8186 bind GL texture 268 and submit 30 triangle vertices, i.e. five SDL quads in one ordered batch. The trace creates texture 268 as RGBA8 1750×1313. In Forest-1 source this is the recurring `Media/levels/waterfall_bottom/cave_background_blur.png` family (IDs 53–55, 59–61), whose PNG has those dimensions; neighboring uploaded assets and the XML draw family corroborate that attribution. The prior query-on replays measured 7473 at 1.272/1.489 ms and 8186 at 1.432/1.553 ms. Those numbers are serialized query-on draw durations, not avoidable cost or query-off FPS.

The candidate source is not itself trivial: its 2,297,750 texels include 1,189,293 alpha-255 (51.76%), 342,116 partial-alpha and 766,341 zero-alpha texels. Its largest all-alpha-255 axis rectangle is source x=317..1444, y=290..1093 (1128×804, 906,912 texels). This source island is **not** a later-overwrite certificate for the five earlier quads; the quads have different transforms and painter ordinals, and SDL batches consecutive copies into one GL call.

## Actual ordered successors, not depth/rectangle intuition

After call 7473 in the scene target, the trace shows only two PixelWobble `fog.png` quads (calls 7507, 7558), one SDL ordinary `tree2_blur.png` quad (7591), and two private fractional-parallax quads (7623, 7678), then the scene-target switch at 7703 and nonlinear Sepia output at 7724. The two private quads carry GL color alpha 0.9490196 and cover only y=589..931 and y=-332..119 respectively. The ordinary `tree2_blur.png` asset has mixed alpha (661,665 zero, 152,287 partial, 289,898 opaque texels); its largest all-opaque axis rectangle is only 157×1487 source pixels, and the active near-left Forest ID7 row is authored alpha 0.7. No later pass is an opaque scene-space replacement: Sepia reads the already-composed scene target. Neither a later quad's bounding box nor the earlier blur's own opaque island permits skipping an earlier fragment.

The trace is sufficient to reject a **large contiguous opaque later-overwrite mask for this expensive terminal ordinary batch**. It does not prove that every possible tiny texel-level overlap is empty. A lossless B would require per-quad order and source-UV/sampler-aware coverage, especially for the five same-texture copies inside the batch, plus physical exact-pixel proof at multiple camera states. The necessary broad overwrite mask is absent in the actual successor sequence, so no production geometry/scissor B was implemented.
