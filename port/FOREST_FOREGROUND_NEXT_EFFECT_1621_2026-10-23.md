# Forest-1 next foreground-facing effect — hard-landing camera shake (16:21, 2026-10-23)

## Finding and importance

A **conditional, still-unported source visual behavior** remains on a forest-1 foreground interaction: the camera's short medium shake when the player lands hard (a fall of at least 700 world units, or downward-dash impact). In forest-1 the authored camera follows X but not Y, so this injects transient horizontal camera displacement; it can move rendered near-player trunks, leaf/shadow layers and the dark lower shrub foreground against the viewport during the 0.30 s shake interval and subsequent follow settling. The port models hard-impact recovery and emits landing material spray but never applies the source's camera shake. This is a bounded *event* discrepancy, not an explanation of the persistent ID37 dark band, ID46 seam, pale middle field, or HUD film overlay. It may be visually salient because near foreground edges move across much of the screen, but this audit did not establish that the delivered 11-second clip contains a qualifying landing or measure pixel coverage.
