# Visual gap source gate — waterfall foreground foliage parallax-phase input (06:44, 2026-10-23)

## Nomination and evidence grade

The strongest distinct **source-proven current draw-path mismatch** found after the latest 2026-10-23 forest/raft/animation/waterfall reports is the **world-space sine input for waterfall-top foreground foliage IDs 64/65**. Their projected quad receives depth parallax, but Linux evaluates foliage sway at the *unparallaxed authored world centre*. Windows evaluates the sine after the ordinary Sprite's depth-parallax world translation. These are wide (2048×677), opaque, actively swaying tree-top quads, so this has a larger potential visible footprint than a small transient; it is a source-path priority, not a measured RGB ranking. Earlier foliage-weather/rotation reports address global time and transforms, not this centre/phase discrepancy. No 2026-10-23 report reviewed owns it. Not blocked or a duplicate of those gates, although the same-family foliage implementation is already in place.

## Exact source-versus-port chain

- `Game/XmlFiles/levels/waterfall_top.xml:1118-1147` authors ordinary `Sprite` IDs 64/65 at `kFarForeground`, 2048×677, alpha 1, `effectfoliagesway`, bottom-corner X intensity 20, local time multiplier 1. ID64 is centred at (-1179.014648, 3660); ID65 at (2059.985352, 3706). The level camera starts at Y=3000 (`:3`). `Game/src/GameObjects/GameObject.cpp:916-924` assigns this depth `(1.1,1.05)` parallax; ordinary GameObjects enable it by default (`Game/src/GameObjects/GameObject.h:317`). In `GameObject.cpp:173-210`, Update computes each axis's `offset=(camera-position)*(multiplier-1)` and puts `position-offset` into World. `Game/ShaderFiles/FoliageSway.fx:40-51` applies World **before** `sin(worldY*.1 + time*local*global)*normalX` (and then the Y sine). Thus source ID64's sine world Y centre at initial camera Y=3000 is 3660 + 0.05*(3660-3000) = **3693**, a 3.3-radian sine-input difference from unshifted centre 3660; ID65's is 3741.3, difference 3.53 radians. The actual vertex Y also includes its local corner displacement, but the phase difference is the same for matching vertices.
- Linux correctly depth-parallaxes the **screen destination** in `port/game/LevelScene.cpp:1231-1255` (`layerX=1.10`, `layerY=1.05`), yet loader `:536-547` stores `sway.worldX/Y=sprite.x/y` once. Draw `:1283-1286` passes that stale authored centre; `port/linux/SDLRenderer.cpp:535-559` passes it to `FoliageQuadVertex`, which constructs shader-world vertices from that centre, and `port/linux/EffectQuadGeometry.h:25-46`/`FoliageSway.h:14-23` evaluate the same sine on those vertices. Camera movement therefore shifts the quad while the port's sway phase lacks the source's corresponding parallax translation. At ID64's 20-world-unit X amplitude, a phase change can alter bottom-edge displacement by up to 40 world units (~26.7 px at 2/3 projection); this is a bound, **not** a measured frame difference.
