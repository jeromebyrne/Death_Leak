## Geometry and draw order

The source contract in `Game/XmlFiles/UI/game_hud.xml:49-65` has 50-unit bars. The ordinary branch in `port/linux/main.cpp:1214-1247` applies scene postprocess, draws film, then the opaque white-tinted `frame_top.png` top and vertically flipped bottom rectangles, and finally calls `hud.Draw()`. `AuthoredUI` projects these to `(0,0,1280,33⅓)` and `(0,686⅔,1280,33⅓)`. The one-row black source texture cannot visually establish vertical flip; source/CPU review supplies that evidence. The separate `sea_2` final-scene 100-pixel bars were not tested here.

Pixel scan of `active-lossless.png` confirms `RGB(0,0,0)` for **every x=0..1279** at rows `0..32` and `687..719`; at center x=640, row 33 is `(8,7,5)` and row 686 is `(1,1,0)`, demonstrating nonblack scene right outside the bands. At x=640, rows 10 and 710 are exact black. The H.264 still has expected compression bleed at boundaries and was not used for exact geometry. The source order, rather than nonoverlapping HUD pixels alone, proves HUD-after-bars ordering. Full dynamic bar/HUD overlap or exact original-Windows compositing was not exercised.
