## Mutation audit and change

- `LevelScene::Draw` had two simulation-visible mutations: each eligible parallax auto-scroll layer advanced `autoScrollYPhase` before viewport culling, and the scene committed `previousRenderedModifiedDelta_` at the end. Calling Draw again for a refresh-only slot therefore changed Waterfall UVs even without an update.
- `LevelScene::PreparePresentation()` now performs the same eligibility-filtered V advance and prior-delta commit exactly once for the fixed update chosen to present. The Linux loop calls it after fixed-update visual/shader/film/HUD preparation, immediately before the scene painter. `LevelScene::Draw` now reads those fields only. The first rendered update still uses zero prior delta; skipped intermediate catch-up updates do not advance V. Repeat draws without another prepare keep the exact sprite state. The Waterfall pixel-wobble V remains unbounded and ordinary repeat V remains wrapped.
- `Combat::AppendPainterItems/Draw`, `WeatherRain::Draw`, and `ParticleSpray::Draw` are const state readers that submit ordered painter commands. Main menu film time, hover and selection change in the update branch; its draw branch reads them. ShaderClock and film selection are prepared before rendering. Renderer GPU state and live frame/capture side effects are separate from game-state mutation; they have **not** been made into a replayable complete frame packet.
- Painter ordering, blend, shader inputs, postprocess, film/HUD, pre-HUD and final capture positions were not moved. Fixed simulation remains 60 Hz; no new refresh-slot loop or 120-FPS claim is made.
