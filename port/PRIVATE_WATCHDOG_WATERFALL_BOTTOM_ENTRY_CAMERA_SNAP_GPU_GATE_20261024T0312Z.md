# Active Waterfall-bottom entry camera snap — scoped source/GPU gate (2026-10-24 03:12Z)

## Verdict and scope

**A first, source-proven composition divergence was found and corrected in the direct LevelTrigger transition path.** The synchronized baseline's first active `waterfall_bottom.xml` frame used the XML seed camera `(0,-2000)` although its `LevelEntry` had placed the player at `(365.985,2405.958)`. Original `GameObjectManager::Initialise` calls `LevelEntry::Initialise` during object initialization, then `Camera2D::FollowObjectsOrigin(player)` before the first destination draw; both bottom-stage follow axes are true. The port's other loading-fade transition already snaps followed axes, but the direct trigger path at `port/linux/main.cpp:1469` had reset camera to `scene.CameraX/Y()` instead. This is not ID22 ground blur, ID27 overdraw, raft registration, or a speculative color correction.

One scoped production edit now initializes followed camera axes from the destination player after `LevelScene::Load` and before `motion.Reset`, preserving authored fixed-camera axes. The edit affects the generic direct trigger route, not loading-fade, door, boat, restart, renderer, Sea-2 AmbientBird or assets. A Docker build and **physical Xorg :0/nouveau NVE7** serial gameplay capture passed. It verifies a material Waterfall-bottom composition change and visible player at the formerly empty sampled moment. It does **not** prove original-Windows pixel parity, later stage camera cadence, audio, or human acceptance.
