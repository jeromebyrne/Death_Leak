# Waterfall-top visual gap: authored vertical water-sheet motion (read-only audit, 2026-10-22)

**One finding.** The port draws the large, layered waterfall curtains as camera-positioned but temporally static textures. Windows scrolls the V coordinate of eight `waterfall_4.png` `ParallaxLayer`s every draw. This removes the principal downward-flow cue from the waterfall-top composition; it is separate from the damage-burst work and was not covered by the inspected `port/*AUDIT*` notes.

**Evidence.** Existing active-play capture `port/build/corrected-gameplay-2026-10-22/corrected-active-gameplay-10s.mp4` reaches `waterfall_top.xml` at frame 398 per adjacent `game.log`; its saved `still-09s.png` shows the broad pale waterfall shaft behind the two broken bridges and orbs. Steam `../store_media/screenshots/16.jpg` shows a qualitatively corresponding shaft/bridge/orb composition, but is a still at a different camera/player state, not a motion or pixel-parity target. `Game/XmlFiles/levels/waterfall_top.xml:109-131,133-155,187-209,261-355,563-585` authors IDs **6, 7, 10, 13, 14, 15, 16, 31** as vertically autoscrolling `waterfall_4.png` layers at differing depth/alpha, with speeds 7, 9 or 11. The original `Game/src/GameObjects/ParallaxLayer.cpp:114-155` advances each layer's texture V by `autoscrollyspeed * 0.001 * (lastUpdateDelta / targetDelta)` from its previous UV, then writes that phase to all four vertices; `:167-175` reads the XML flags/speeds. The port `port/game/LevelScene.cpp:357-364` parses follow-camera and camera-parallax multipliers but no autoscroll fields; `:902-909` supplies UV shifts only from camera position. These eight layers have zero camera-Y parallax and `followcamy=false`, so their texture V stays fixed in the port. A source-tree search found no `autoscroll` implementation in `port/game`, `port/render`, or `port/linux`.

**Bounded follow-up.** In a separate renderer-owned change, retain per-layer autoscroll-Y flag, speed and accumulated V phase; advance at the source's update-delta/target-delta ratio and apply that phase through the existing whole-quad parallax UV path (including the several alpha/depth layers). Preserve authored XML, art, static quad placement and painter order. Once the GPU lane is free, compare the same waterfall-top camera/route in two time-separated *active gameplay* frames and check that the curtains flow at their distinct source-relative rates without UV seams; then request human visual review.

**Limits.** This is a source-proven motion-path gap, not a measured Windows-versus-Linux pixel match or visual acceptance. The Steam still cannot validate direction or rate; the saved port still alone cannot demonstrate temporal stasis, which follows from the port code path. No source/build/game/GPU operation was performed in this audit.
