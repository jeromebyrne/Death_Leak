## Verdict

The fractional-parallax float draw's alpha single-apply fix passes the focused source/CPU gate, two isolated Docker production builds, and a controlled physical-Xorg/NVE7 same-frame A/B. At Forest-1 `R:180`, the corrected full frame makes the authored black ID37 near-ground shrub contribution modestly darker without moving its first visible row (y596).
