## Critical reference-normalization observation

The saved `../store_media/screenshots/14.jpg` is **1920×1080**, but inspection of its RGB rows shows the pictured content begins at row **50** and ends at row **1029**; rows above/below are black letterbox bands with JPEG bleed within about two rows of the edges. This is a 1920×980 visible-content aperture inside a 1920×1080 file. The current `port/build/forest14-current-head-2026-10-22/forest-r110.png` is 1280×720 without corresponding solid top/bottom bars. For a first registration, scale the **whole** Steam JPEG uniformly by 2/3 to 1280×720 and **mask** approximately y=0–33 and y=687–719; preserve the source content geometry. Cropping the 980-px-high content and stretching it to 720 px would use Y scale 0.735 instead of X scale 0.667 (about **10.2% vertical stretch**) and turn the raft/branch silhouette into a false mismatch. Nor should bars be treated as missing Linux art. The bars' origin (game cinematic mask versus Steam processing) is not established by the JPEG.

The image is a qualitative forest-1 reference: distinctive left raft, ID117 diagonal pickup under the lower right branch, upper branch/orb column and broad right trunk. Its HUD reads 13 orbs/reduced health versus the saved R:110 Linux frame's 3/full health. The 30-second saved trailer has no matching forest-1 sequence (`port/MATCHED_FOREST_REFERENCE_SEARCH_2026-10-22.md`); screenshot 13 is less identifiable; screenshot 14 does not show right-exit ID15 (`port/FOREST_ID15_MATCHED_REFERENCE_2026-10-22.md`).
