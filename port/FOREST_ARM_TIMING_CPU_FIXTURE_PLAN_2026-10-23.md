# Forest player back-arm prior-rendered-delta CPU fixture plan — 2026-10-23

## Verdict and boundary

Read-only source/dirty-tree audit, plus this report only. The forest ID2 attack arm has its own fractional clock and, on a successful shot, two finite-animation advances in one update. In the current port both consume current `dt` (`port/game/LevelScene.cpp:1163-1177`); the Windows `AnimationPart::Animate` calls consume `Timing::GetLastUpdateDelta()` (`Game/src/GameObjects/Player.cpp:467-475`, `Character.cpp:1098-1111`, `AnimationPart.cpp:201-237`). Windows initializes that slot to zero and writes the modified update delta *after* Render (`Game/src/Core/Timing.cpp:6-16`, `Game/src/program.cpp:149-177`). This is a source-backed timing discrepancy, not a measured RGB rank, matched Windows frame, or runtime parity claim.

`forest_exploration_1.xml:33-51` authors Player ID2 and `player_anim_default.xml:1483-1514` gives ten Still and ten Running attack textures, each at 30 fps. The original draws the unfinished arm behind the body (`Character.cpp:1888-1907`); the port loads only these two arm sequences at `LevelScene.cpp:439-463` and emits the arm before the body at `:1307-1314`. Keep the scope to this arm. The existing `FOREST_ANIMATION_PRECEDING_DELTA_CPU_SPY_2026-10-23.md` covers body/orb texture cadence; `FOREST_ANIMATION_DIRTY_TREE_INTEGRATION_MAP_2026-10-23.md` and `FOREST_PRIOR_RENDERED_DELTA_IMPLEMENTATION_2026-10-23.md` reserve their process-owned previous-render slot and integration. Do not create another clock or duplicate their 20-tick body/orb test.
