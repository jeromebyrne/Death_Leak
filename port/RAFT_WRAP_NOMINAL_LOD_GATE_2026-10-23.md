# Raft WRAP nominal-LOD gate — 2026-10-23

## Bounded verdict

The Pillow BOX level-3/4 WRAP-versus-CLAMP alpha differences are **real asset/fixture sensitivity, but unlikely to contribute to the delivered Forest-1 raft at its authored native gameplay scale**. An ordinary raft projects to 305.532 × 83.544 pixels from a 1024 × 280 texture: 3.35153 source texels per output pixel on either axis, or implicit LOD approximately **1.745**. With the private path's trilinear minifier, that nominal LOD blends levels 1 and 2, **not** levels 3/4. The BOX fixture's outer alpha is zero at levels 1/2, so their U-edge WRAP/CLAMP raw-RGB differences should not change straight-alpha compositing. This is a prediction, not a measured GPU LOD or proof of zero framebuffer pixels. More importantly, the delivered 11-second clip documents the experiment as disabled, but its effective process environment is unproven; no GL-raft draw in that MP4 can be asserted.

## Quantitative visibility bound

The draw is affine and orthographic, so the nominal texture-space footprint is `1024/305.532389 = 3.3515268` U texels/pixel and `280/83.544011 = 3.3515269` V texels/pixel; `log2(max) = 1.74482`. No perspective, camera zoom, parallax or authored UV repeat is applied to these three sprites in this renderer path. Under ordinary trilinear selection only levels 1/2 carry weight at this LOD. To give **level 3** positive trilinear weight requires LOD > 2, i.e. projected dimensions below about 256 × 70 (more than 16.2% smaller than authored); for **level 4** positive weight requires LOD > 3, below about 128 × 35 (more than 58.1% smaller). The active native-window route provides no such shrinkage. At level 3 the half-texel U edge band would project to ~1.19 screen pixels and at level 4 ~2.39 pixels if those levels were selected; at levels 1/2 it is only ~0.30/~0.60 pixel. These are boundary *support* widths, not guaranteed rasterized or visible pixels.

`port/RAFT_WRAP_CPU_PIXEL_FIXTURE_2026-10-23.md` gives the explicit BOX alpha oracle: levels 0–2 have transparent outer columns/rows; at level 3 a selected U-edge probe changes alpha 10→5, and at level 4 62→31. Therefore the larger BOX alpha differences require deeper selected levels (or actual GPU-generated level-1/2 alpha edge content different from this fixture). Even at a deeper LOD, actual pixel-center UV, overdraw, blending, sepia, video encoding, and whether the opt-in branch ran determine delivered visibility. An existing Linux **mip-off versus mip-on** R:110 physical-GPU A/B changed many raft-region pixels (`port/FOREST_RAFT_MIP_SAME_STATE_GPU_DISCRIMINATOR_2026-10-23.md`), but it changed mip filtering as well as path and did **not** isolate CLAMP versus WRAP or establish clip provenance/Windows parity.
