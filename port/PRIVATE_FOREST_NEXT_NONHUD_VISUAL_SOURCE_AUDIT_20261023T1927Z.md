# Private active-Forest non-HUD visual source audit — 2026-10-23 19:27 UTC

## One nominated gap

**Player ID2 never renders the authored soft/hard impact-landing recovery poses, although Linux already classifies and times those landings.** On a qualifying drop (strictly >300 and <700 world units for soft, >=700 for hard) or downward-dash landing, the original selects a conspicuous crouched `ImpactLandSoft` or `ImpactLandHard` body sequence ahead of ordinary idle/run/air states. At current HEAD `ce8e7ed`, Linux `PlayerMotion` records `ImpactRecovery::Soft/Hard`, but the Forest player animation loader omits both sequences and `UpdatePlayerVisual` never selects either; after landing it can display `Still`/`Running` instead. This is a conditional focal-silhouette difference, **not** a claim that the existing 10–11-second clip includes such a landing or that it explains the persistent dark/pale scenery bands. Its priority is source certainty and whole-player pose during a repeatable traversal event, not measured Windows pixel area.

This refines, rather than reopens, the 2026-10-22 `RIGHT_STICK_FALL_GATE_SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-22.md`: that report established the impact flags and cited the two original sequences, while the current port has since implemented motion recovery and hard-landing camera shake (`FOREST_HARD_LANDING_SHAKE_CPU_IMPLEMENTATION_2026-10-23.md`). The remaining *visual body-selection* omission is distinct from the completed crouch, ordinary falling-body delay, jump/landing material spray, and shake work. `PRIVATE_CROUCH_CURRENT_HEAD_GPU_GATE_20261023T1847Z.md` confirms crouch already renders; it does not cover impact poses. Prior broad Forest effect inventories were negative for missing scene-effect classes, not for this conditional Player ID2 animation path.

## Original-to-port evidence

- Forest-1 authors an always-updated Player ID2 using `XmlFiles\\animation\\player_anim_default.xml` (`Game/XmlFiles/levels/forest_exploration_1.xml:33-46`). That file declares `ImpactLandSoft` at 30 FPS with six individual `duck_default_005`→`000` frame textures (`Game/XmlFiles/animation/player_anim_default.xml:767-812`) and `ImpactLandHard` at 15 FPS with a longer held-then-rising duck sequence (`:814-894`). These are individual frame PNGs, not atlas cells.
- Original landing classification is in `Game/src/GameObjects/Character.cpp:319-379`: a successful roll cancels impact flags; qualifying dry, nonrolling drops set short/hard recovery, and `DoLargeImpactLanding` also starts camera shake (`:124-135`). A retained downward-dash landing can independently invoke hard impact (`:198-212`). `Character::UpdateAnimations` gives hard impact absolute body priority, animates `ImpactLandHard` and returns; soft selects/animates `ImpactLandSoft` and returns until finished (`:735-790`). `AnimationPart::Animate` advances at most one frame per update using its update-delta slot (`Game/src/Animation/AnimationPart.cpp:201-235`), and completion is frame-index based (`:47-54`).
- Linux has the event state: `port/game/PlayerMotion.cpp:14-15,53-55,199-218` uses 6/30 and 11/15 second recovery timers and chooses Soft/Hard on qualifying contact; `port/game/PlayerMotion.h:20-21` exposes `Recovery()`/`ImpactRecovering()`. The live loop consumes a hard-landing event for camera shake (`port/linux/main.cpp:810-816`) and passes ordinary motion state to `scene.UpdatePlayerVisual` (`:1249`), **but not recovery kind**. The loader's explicit body-sequence whitelist contains Crouch/Roll/etc. but neither impact name (`port/game/LevelScene.cpp:408-440`); the visual selection chain begins with melee/roll/crouch and then ordinary air/run/idle (`:1211-1234`). A targeted source search of `port/game` and `port/linux` found no `ImpactLandSoft/Hard` use in this draw path; `port/game/Combat.cpp:166` merely excludes these names in another parser and does not render them.
