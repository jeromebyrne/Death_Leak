## Registration caveat and source decision

The current HUD film overlay advances from wall-clock milliseconds (`port/linux/main.cpp:1396-1403`), so equal simulation arguments alone **do not** guarantee identical final pixels. The first `hide-10` run and a repeat baseline differed broadly outside either target layer (unaffected crop `[50,100,450,500)` changed by 126,753 pixels), an overlay-phase confound; those naive direct-difference numbers are **not** causal evidence. Phase A captures above were selected by exact equality of that distant control crop. The separate `hide-10-37-2` control was repeated until it matched phase A (third attempt), and its whole-frame difference versus `hide-10-37` was confined to the player bbox. Unmatched controls remain in evidence but are excluded from the quantitative conclusion.

Original Forest XML authors ID10 `tree2_blur.png` at `kMiddleForeground`, alpha `.7`, fade disabled, after player ID2; original shader/blend is alpha-over and the current SDL ordinary draw applies quantized tint alpha once, as sourced in `PRIVATE_FOREST_PLAYER_LEGIBILITY_SOURCE_GATE_20261023T1942Z.md`. ID37 is authored `.95` alpha lower `shrubbery.png` and has no player-fade rule. The clean ID10/ID37 controls confirm **which Linux layers** cause the low contrast; they do not show a departure from those original commands. A state-matched original-Windows lossless draw packet (ID2/10/37 order, transform/UV, alpha/blend, texture/sampler, camera, animation/film phase, same postprocess boundary) remains required to establish any renderer regression. The Steam screenshot and asynchronous MP4 still cannot supply that comparison. No opacity/order/brightness/mip patch was made.
