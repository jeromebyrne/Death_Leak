## Compared contract

- `Game/XmlFiles/levels/waterfall_top.xml:261-307` authors IDs 13/14 as adjacent `kGroundBack` `ParallaxLayer`s, both `Media/waterfall_4.png`, opacity **0.3**, horizontal flip true, vertical flip false, `effectpixelwobble` intensity **0.005** and speed modifier **10**, vertical autoscroll speed **11**. Their equal 1621.325806-unit heights and center-Y separation of about 1620.999 units create only an approximately 0.327-unit overlap; order matters at that thin seam, not over a broad common surface.
- Windows `Game/ShaderFiles/BasicLightTexture_PixelWobble.fx:56-64` perturbs V then U, samples RGBA with wrapped U/V and `MIN_LINEAR_MAG_POINT_MIP_LINEAR` (`:13-19`), multiplies **sample alpha only** by object alpha, and returns the original sampled RGB. `Game/src/GameObjects/Sprite.cpp:675-710` supplies the authored alpha/intensity/speed and vertex buffer. Windows enables source-alpha-over RGB blending through `Game/src/Core/Graphics.cpp:380-407`; depth comparison is `ALWAYS` (`:80-107`), so its depth buffer does not mask a later translucent peer.
- The Windows manager renders descending depth with stable `std::list::sort` (`Game/src/GameObjects/GameObjectManager.cpp:58-64,302-329,1022-1025`). The Linux scene assigns parsed depth and XML order, then `std::stable_sort`s descending (`port/game/LevelScene.cpp:450-453,576`), and draws in that order (`:951-955,1028`). Thus ID13 precedes ID14 at equal depth in both, including their narrow overlap.
- Linux `port/game/LevelScene.cpp:504-517,999-1013` carries wobble parameters, authored alpha (also tint alpha for ordinary copies), and flips into the draw sprite. `port/linux/SDLRenderer.cpp:208-212,317-351,356-369,609-620` selects the pixel-wobble GL branch, samples RGBA with the same V-then-U equations and an alpha-only `spriteAlpha` multiplier, sets `GL_SRC_ALPHA, GL_ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA`, `GL_REPEAT`, linear minification and nearest magnification, and flushes queued SDL copies before drawing. That branch uses `sprite.opacity`, **not** the quantized `tint.a`; it therefore avoids double-applying the authored 0.3 alpha. Its `glDisable(GL_DEPTH_TEST)` is consistent for RGB composition with Windows' `DepthFunc=ALWAYS` painter order.

The known exception remains: Linux GL corners ignore `uvShiftY` at `port/linux/SDLRenderer.cpp:356-369`, while the Windows pixel-wobble shader consumes the scrolled vertex UVs (`Game/src/GameObjects/ParallaxLayer.cpp:114-155`). This is the finding already isolated in `port/WATERFALL_WOBBLE_UV_IMPLEMENTATION_AUDIT_2026-10-22.md`, not a second blend mismatch. Windows' separate framebuffer-alpha blend factors (`Graphics.cpp:401-403`) differ from ordinary GL alpha-buffer behavior, but no source-backed evidence here shows that destination alpha changes the final waterfall RGB; the relevant source/destination **RGB** factors match. A broad opacity/order correction would therefore be speculative.
