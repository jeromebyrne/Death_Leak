## Actual painter and blend contract

`LevelScene` stable-sorts descending depth and draws XML objects in authored order within a depth. Fog ScrollingSprite IDs 117/118/119/121 are the **last four kGroundFront (1750)** objects. They draw after the kPlayer (3000) body, orbs and prior kGroundFront objects. Later kFarForeground (1500) and kMiddleForeground (1000) objects are patchy/partially transparent sprites, particles or alpha-modulated sheets, not a guaranteed opaque fullscreen overwrite. Thus neither ground/front ancestry nor a hypothetical later ground layer proves their fragments hidden. The ordinary `SpriteMayIntersectViewport` rotated-quad admission already rejects wholly offscreen fog; prior ID117 physical fringe test showed why unrotated culling is unsafe (`port/ROTATED_FOG_CULL_GPU_REVIEW_2026-10-22.md`).

All four fog sheets have authored 1988×1290 rotated native quads, angle 0.11 rad, PixelWobble intensity 0.1/speed 0.1, independent X scroll/fade, and different flips; source opacity is 0.6/0.6/0.6/0.5. `DrawPixelWobble` flushes SDL, binds a private repeat/mip texture when available, enables `GL_SRC_ALPHA, GL_ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA`, and draws a rotated GL quad. The fragment shader's **float** `spriteAlpha` is `sprite.opacity`, not the rounded `tint.a`: `src.a = sampled.a * sprite.opacity`. Under this blend the destination is unchanged only when source alpha is exactly zero (or identical source/destination by coincidence, which cannot be guaranteed). In particular, a rounded byte alpha of zero during early fade **does not** justify skipping a PixelWobble draw: the float opacity may already be positive.

The 1988×1290 `fog.png` has 1,355,051 nonzero-alpha texels (52.84%), 1,209,469 zero texels, and 174,174 alpha-255 texels. Zero texels are not a static screen mask: the fragment shader perturbs V then U with time-dependent sine, wraps/repeats the texture and minifies/filter samples. The four independently moving/rotated/flipped sheets overlap, so their nonzero alpha contributes in painter order. No pixel-independent full-draw or rectangular scissor mask can be proved from the texture's 47.16% zero texels. `waterfall_4.png` has 458,485 nonzero-alpha texels of 524,288 (87.45%) and **zero** alpha-255 texels. Its measured PixelWobble sheets therefore cannot supply an opaque fog occluder; authored alpha 0.2/0.3 and other later sheets further prevent an overwrite proof.
