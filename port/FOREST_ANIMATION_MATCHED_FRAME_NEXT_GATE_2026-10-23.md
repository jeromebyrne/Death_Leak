Even after obtaining a Windows EXE and D3D10-capable host, ordinary screenshots or X11/desktop recordings cannot establish identical animation phase. The original loop is Update → Render → Present → commit modified `LastUpdateDelta`; `AnimationPart` consumes that prior value, with one frame maximum per call. ID68 only updates while eligible in its 4000×4000 object zone and can draw its frozen texture while off-zone; ID2 body sequences reset on transition; a successful shot restarts and advances the finite arm twice on the same prior slot. The port's `--frames`, `--fixed-dt`, `--capture` and `--camera-script` do not supply arbitrary same-history Windows state or a full same-render sidecar (`FOREST1_MATCHED_FRAME_CAPTURE_PACKET_2026-10-23.md`). The physical still/MP4 ordinals cannot be registered to the CPU spy's modified-delta/eligibility/sequence schedule. A stock shipped EXE plus an unsynchronized frame grab remains qualitative only unless a validated hook/debugger supplies the internal state and emitted draw texture paths.
