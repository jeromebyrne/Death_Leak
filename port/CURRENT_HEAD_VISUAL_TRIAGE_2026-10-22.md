# Current-HEAD visual triage: player-knife launch height

## Decision

At inspected Git HEAD `4661632` plus the present uncommitted right-stick aim/fire wiring, the best **distinct, bounded, source-demonstrable active-gameplay composition correction** outside the already assigned parallax UV, fog mip, camera-follow lag, aim-line, shaft/filter, and waterfall-effect reports is the **player knife's state-dependent launch Y**. Windows starts a knife at player Y +25 normally, Y+0 while crouching, Y+90 while ascending, or Y+65 while descending (the latter two assume not crouched). Linux always starts at Y+25. Thus the knife originates 65 world units (~43.3 output px at 2/3 scale) too low on an ascending shot, or 40 world units (~26.7 px) too low on descent. The mismatch affects the first visible knife position and subsequent trajectory/impact composition, not the authored scenery. This is a priority among newly isolated *one-owner, live-input-relevant* visual slices, not a measured image-area ranking above the separately reported aim line or fog/foreground effects.

## Evidence and boundary

- Original `Game/src/GameObjects/Player.cpp:97-98,424-444` sets the projectile attachment offset `(5,25)`, applies crouch Y−25, then adds 65 for `mIsMidAirMovingUp` or 40 for `mIsMidAirMovingDown`; it applies the ±5 X offset **twice**, yielding the same ±10 X already used by the port. The up/down flags are mutually selected and cleared in `Game/src/GameObjects/Character.cpp:290-316`. This is a source launch-center contract, not a speculative screenshot interpretation.
- Current `port/game/Combat.cpp:392-408` correctly uses normalized right-stick aim for velocity and ±10 X, but initializes every player shot at `y+25.f` with no crouch/airborne state. `port/linux/main.cpp:687-702` now supplies right-stick aim and calls `Combat::Update` with `grounded` and `rolling`, but does not pass crouch or ascending/descending state. Source fire is allowed during ordinary airborne play (`Player.cpp:388-414` gates wall-side, roll and water, not air); the new live aim route therefore exposes the origin error. The most recent right-stick implementation report mentions projectile origin as an excluded remainder but does not specify or implement this slice.
- The newest saved active forest gameplay evidence is `port/build/current-head-visual-capture-2026-10-22/forest-1-current-head-active-10s.mp4` and its 1/5/9-second stills, documented by `port/CURRENT_HEAD_VISUAL_CAPTURE_2026-10-22.md`. That binary predates HEAD and the current unbuilt right-stick work; the stills show active forest composition, **not** a matched knife-launch diagnostic. `port/build/forest14-current-head-2026-10-22/forest-r110.png` and Steam `../store_media/screenshots/14.jpg` are also unmatched pose/state. No observed pixel displacement is claimed from these images. Waterfall screenshot 16 is a different scene and cannot validate this forest shot.
