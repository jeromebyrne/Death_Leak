# Waterfall-top ~20 s paired-raft water UV / pixel-wobble source audit

## Result and boundary

**First source-proven shader-input divergence: IDs 13/14 lose the integer part of their accumulating Y-scroll phase before the pixel-wobble shader's second sine.** This is a current-source contract difference that can alter horizontally sampled water pixels after the first V wrap; it is **not** a measured same-state Windows-vs-Linux pixel difference at present 711. Ordinary sheets 6/7/31 do not have that nonlinear shader dependency, so their integer-V removal is wrap-equivalent for texture sampling. No splash layer-order, Waterfall-bottom, or other scene ownership is revisited.

## Source trace

- `Game/XmlFiles/levels/waterfall_top.xml:109-160,261-307,563-585` authors 6/7/13/14/31 as horizontally flipped, vertically unflipped, Y-auto-scrolling `waterfall_4.png` sheets, with speeds 7/11/11/11/7 and alpha .5/.2/.3/.3/.2 respectively. Only 13/14 select `effectpixelwobble` (`Intensity=.005`, `SpeedMod=10`); the others use ordinary `effectlighttexture`.
- Windows `Game/src/GameObjects/ParallaxLayer.cpp:110-155` sets `yShift = m_vertices[0].TexCoord.y + speed*.001*(lastUpdateDelta/targetDelta)` on each draw, writes `yShift` and `yShift+1` into the vertices, and does **not** modulo or floor the phase. `Game/src/GameObjects/Sprite.cpp:675-709` submits that vertex buffer to the wobble effect. `Game/ShaderFiles/BasicLightTexture_PixelWobble.fx:55-65` adds a U-dependent perturbation to V, then computes `U += sin(modifiedV * Time * SpeedMod) * Intensity`, then uses wrap sampling. Wrap addressing is applied at the final sample, **after** the nonlinear second sine.
- Current Linux `port/game/LevelScene.cpp:1410-1451` accumulates the preceding rendered delta with the authored speed but executes `autoScrollYPhase -= floor(autoScrollYPhase)` before assigning `draw.uvShiftY`. `port/linux/WobbleUV.h:7-15`, `EffectQuadGeometry.h:12-26`, and `SDLRenderer.cpp:646-700` forward this reduced shift into the actual pixel-wobble quad. Its GLSL at `SDLRenderer.cpp:1094-1107` preserves V-then-U sine order and performs `1-V` at final sampling; the earlier missing-shift defect is closed in the current tree. The dirty renderer diff adds a conditional raft mip experiment, not a change to this wobble path.

Let the source's unbounded phase be `p=n+f` with positive integer `n`. For an otherwise identical corner/pixel and effect clock, both texture samplers wrap V to the same `f`, but the U perturbations are `.005*sin((baseV+n+f+firstWobble)*Time*10)` (Windows) versus `.005*sin((baseV+f+firstWobble)*Time*10)` (Linux). These are generally unequal because `Time*10` is not constrained to an integer multiple of `2π`. For a simple nondegenerate illustrative input `baseV=0`, first wobble=0, `p=1.44`, and `Time*SpeedMod=10`, the U terms are approximately `+.0048283` and `-.0047580` (difference `.0095863` of a texture span); **this is a formula witness, not the recorded 20 s frame state**. Original `m_vertices[0]` has no modulo, while port phase first wraps after roughly 137 nominal 60-Hz Waterfall-top draws at scaled speed 11 (~2.27 s); the synchronized top interval lasts ~12 s, so this branch is relevant to the paired-raft phase, conditional on the level's actual draw history.
