## Discriminating geometry and media evidence

- `Game/XmlFiles/levels/forest_exploration_1.xml` places the unique ID117 pickup at world X **1167.985** and ID15 at X **2796.985**, **1629** world units farther right. ID15 is a centered 512×512 quad, rotation **−1.469999 rad**, depth `kGroundFront`; ID13 right exit is at X **3410.985**. `Game/src/GameObjects/GameObject.cpp:890-919` gives `kGroundFront` and pickup/ground sprites horizontal parallax multiplier **1.0**. `Game/src/Core/Camera2D.cpp` uses an orthographic viewport; XML camera zoom is **1**. Thus the horizontal world separation remains roughly 1629 screen pixels at native projection, whereas ID15's rotated quad extends at most `256(|cos θ|+|sin θ|)` ≈ **280 px** left of its center. Exact historical viewport/camera are unknown, so these are a geometry discriminator, not pixel-registration metadata.
- Screenshot `../store_media/screenshots/14.jpg` (1920×1080 JPEG) visibly centers the raft, long branch, ID117 pickup and orb cluster. At the pickup's approximate displayed X≈900/1200 (≈1440/1920 native), projecting ID15 at the same camera puts its center ≈**3069 px** and its leftmost geometric edge ≈**2789 px**, beyond the 1920-wide image. Even a generous landmark-position error cannot bridge that gap. Hence screenshot 14 does **not** show an ID15 edge to judge. Its pale background/ground haze must not be labeled ID15. This refines—not contradicts—the prior finding that screenshot 14 matches forest-1.
- Screenshot `13.jpg` is forest-like but lacks the unique ID117/ID15 right-exit landmarks and a demonstrated ID15 isolated shape/camera; it cannot be promoted to a matched ID15 reference. Screenshot `15.jpg` is an outdoor combat scene, not this forest shaft. The saved `../store_media/steam-trailer-reference-30s.mp4` was previously audited as open/scaffold action without the screenshot-14 forest landmarks. These observations cover the locally saved store media, **not all public footage**.
