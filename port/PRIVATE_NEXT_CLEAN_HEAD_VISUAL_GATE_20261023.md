## Decision

**One high-confidence, locally isolatable source-path visual mismatch: the active HUD orb-count typeface.** The original requests a 32-unit **Impact** D3DX font; clean HEAD `e10084a` draws the count with its renderer-neutral 5×7 block-glyph routine at scale 3. The digits `2` at 1 s and `5` at 5/9 s are present in the clean-HEAD Forest-1 stills, so this is an observed active-gameplay element, not a hypothetical class. The mismatch is the *font/rasterization path and glyph design*, not a measured original-Windows pixel error or a claim that the count, color, or anchor is wrong. It is small in area and should not be mistaken for the large lower-ground contrast concern. No edit is justified from the unmatched Steam JPEG.
