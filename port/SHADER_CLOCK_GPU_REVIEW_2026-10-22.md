# Process shader-clock continuity and physical GPU review — 2026-10-22

## Change and source contract

Implemented the bounded clock slice from `SHADER_CLOCK_CONTINUITY_AUDIT_2026-10-22.md`. `ShaderClock` is owned once by `port/linux/main.cpp`, outside `LevelScene` and weather. Every main-loop update advances float total shader time by the port's frame delta, including menu and modal frames and transition iterations that skip drawing. A render advances a separate pixel-wobble oscillator with the **previous rendered** frame's delta; strict `>10` and `<5` thresholds switch direction. `RenderCompleted` stores the current delta after present. This follows `Game/src/program.cpp:169-177` and `Game/src/Game.cpp:371-401` ordering. The port currently has no Windows-style damage time modifier; the existing frame delta is the only available modified-delta equivalent.

`LevelScene::Draw` receives an explicit clock snapshot and gives process total to foliage, oscillator time to pixel wobble. `Load` and `UpdateVisuals` no longer own or reset those two shader clocks. Weather's distinct global foliage phase multiplier and all scene-local animation/particle timers remain untouched. This also leaves an explicit total-time seam for future vertex-wobble/noise/UI effect ports; those effects are **not** implemented here.
