# Fog PixelWobble mip-filter physical-GPU gate — 2026-10-22

## Verdict

**Gate passed after a narrowly scoped live-GL bug fix.** The initial `89c9f50` build silently erased the fog on physical `nouveau / NVE7`: at the reproducible forest-1 frame 300 its full-scene BMP was byte-identical to the fog-hidden control. SDL had left `GL_UNPACK_ROW_LENGTH=50`; the private 1988×1290 PNG upload only reset unpack alignment, so readback of the uploaded center texel was `(0,0,0,0)` while the decoded source center was `(220,220,220,255)`. GL reported no error. The fix resets unpack row length, skipped rows/pixels and PBO binding for the private upload, then restores every previous value. Physical readback in the isolated diagnostic changed to `(221,221,221,255)` at the center.

The repaired build renders the fog through the new private `GL_LINEAR_MIPMAP_LINEAR` / `GL_NEAREST` PixelWobble path. At fixed frame 300 the fog-versus-hidden full scene changes **137,138 pixels** in `[0,213,747,720)`, mean absolute RGB **4.657/255**, maximum channel difference 106. The pre-mip `ae488cc` baseline versus repaired mip baseline changes **164,999 pixels** in the fog region `[0,213,807,720)`, mean absolute RGB **2.313/255**, maximum channel difference 81. The hidden-fog frames are **byte-identical** between binaries, excluding a broad non-fog scene regression at this frame. Fog-only frames change 183,113 pixels. The new mip look is materially different; it is not asserted to match a Windows frame.
