They demonstrate why full-RGBA reconstruction alone does not prove filtered/rotated runtime pixel identity.

## Disposition

Accept only the opt-in orange-light coverage slice and full-RGBA packer feature. Do not claim batching, FPS improvement, broad all-PNG coverage, 60 FPS, 8K/high-Hz or release readiness. Next safe expansion should target a co-resident ordinary nearest-sampled family and establish both exact full-frame pixels and real repeated GL bind/submission reduction; minified/rotated families need a separate normalized-UV precision fix before mapping.
