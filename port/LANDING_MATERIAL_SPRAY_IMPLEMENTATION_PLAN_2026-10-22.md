# Landing material spray — implementation/test plan (2026-10-22)

## Contract and ownership

- Source: `Game/src/GameObjects/Character.cpp:336-342,409-490`. Measure **tracked airborne apex center Y minus resolved landing center Y** (world Y is up), not speed, airtime, frame displacement, or height above takeoff. Source records its high point while its midair-up/down flags are active, then handles material effects on solid contact **before** buffered-roll/impact handling. The Linux slice should track a valid airborne apex and reset it on landing and `PlayerMotion::Reset`; do not let an authored initial placement or `LevelEntry` sentinel (`-99999`) create a fake first landing. Check a real ledge fall as well as controlled trajectories because the source midair-state gate is not identical to merely `!grounded_`.
- `PlayerMotion` owns event detection, once per **airborne-to-grounded** resolution in `Update` (`port/game/PlayerMotion.cpp:121-172`), after the selected contact and final `y_` are known. Carry `wasGrounded`, apex validity/highest Y, resolved `groundedSegment_`, landing `(x_, y_)`, and `facing_`; call a new `LevelScene::EmitLandingMaterialSpray(segment, x, y, facing, drop)` before any roll/impact response. Initialize/clear tracking for new airborne episodes; avoid emitting on sustained ground, an upward underside collision, or a no-contact frame. Do not alter collision selection, jump, dash, or roll physics.
- `LevelScene` owns material/texture lookup, spray construction, random stream, lifetime update/erase, and draw alongside the player at `Depth("kPlayer")`. Give landing a distinct `landingSprays_` vector, accessor/test spy, and RNG state; clear them on `Load`. Extend the existing footstep/takeoff update and player draw seams (`LevelScene.cpp:836-843,945-1012,1234-1237`) without folding landing into either effect's state. Prefer a small shared factory only if it preserves each effect's different parameters and RNG order. `ParticleSpray.width=1200,height=720` is the culling box, not a visible quad.

## Eligibility and exact variants

The selected contact must be a nonvertical `SolidLineStrip` segment with a material whose particle texture loaded successfully (`LevelScene.cpp:152-163,258-274`); `groundedSegment_ == size_t(-1)` means box/smashable contact and is ineligible. Preserve the existing highest-contact/tie result: do not substitute a nearby textured line when a box won. Missing material, empty particle list, missing file, or invalid texture produce no visual spray. Water by itself is not a landing surface; do not add a broad `inWater_` veto to a genuinely selected textured solid line, since the source material gate is line-based. A downward dash only qualifies if it actually ends on an eligible line. A pre-landing buffered roll does not suppress the spray. No audio/vibration is in this visual slice.

| `>=700` | One **10-particle** spray | `(landingX + facing*10, landingY - 214.898743/2)` | direction `(0,1)`, spread `.4`, speed `3–5`, lifetime `.4–1 s`, size `5–10`, gravity `.8`, scale-to `15` |

Both variants: selected material texture, player depth, culling box `1200x720`, nonlooping, brightness `.8–1`, loop-time `1`, scales-by-lifetime, spawn spread `(5,.2)`, fade-in `.15`, fade-out `.9`. Match source factory quantization: nonnegative random X addition `0..spread` with corresponding Y subtraction, signed integer spawn X `0..50` and Y `0..2`, descending lifetimes by `(max-min)/count` per index. Use a private deterministic Linux RNG; do not require identity with Windows' global RNG. The currently loaded Forest-1 `grassground` material resolves to `rock_particle.png` (`forest_exploration_1.xml:33-85`, `materials.xml:173-179`). Note the source's animation soft-impact check uses `>300`, but **visual spray begins at exactly 300**.

## CPU acceptance checks (no GPU ownership in this plan)

1. Use a deterministic scene/ground fixture with a loaded `grassground` line and controlled apex-to-resolved-center drops **299.9, 300, 699.9, 700**. Assert landing event counts/particle counts **0, 1x5, 1x5, 1x10**, respectively, on first contact and no further emission over grounded frames. For exact boundary arithmetic, expose/seed the motion apex in a narrow test seam or construct known coordinates; do not equate nominal spawn height to measured drop after physics integration.
2. Inspect new `LandingSprays()` (or a scene event spy) for both origins, `kPlayer` depth, material texture, culling dimensions, flags, fade/scale values, and per-particle ranges/descending lifetimes. Assert left-facing X offset as well as right-facing. Fixed seed makes range/sequence checks repeatable; it is not a Windows-pixel-RNG parity claim.
3. Negative controls at `>=700`: solid-box winner (including line/box overlap or equal-height tie), smashable top, textured line not yet contacted, missing/empty material texture, and authored-entry initial snap all yield zero. Verify no duplicate on a sustained ground contact or after a brief slope/segment change without genuine airborne drop. Check actual ledge-fall tracking and a pre-landing roll-buffer case; the latter must still emit.
4. Regression non-overlap: jump rising edge still owns the existing 10-particle **takeoff** spray (`PlayerMotion.cpp:67-77`), Running animation frame transition still owns its 3-particle **footstep** spray (`LevelScene.cpp:979-1012,1087`), and landing neither consumes their RNG streams nor changes their counts, contact timing, or draw visibility. Inspect each dedicated vector separately rather than counting all particles in a frame.
