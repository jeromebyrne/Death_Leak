## New source contract and bounded evidence

A valid coordinate is therefore insufficient evidence for a body submission.
- Original player alpha can be zero for at least two non-occlusion reasons: active teleport in `Character.cpp:149–162`, or death in `Player.cpp:190–196`. Authored `waterfall_bottom.xml:9–17` starts player at alpha 1 and disables ordinary occlusion-fade, but runtime state can supersede authored alpha. This is a **conditional contract**, not a claim that death/teleport occurred in the 25 s packet.
- Linux `LevelScene::Draw` applies `SpriteMayIntersectViewport(draw)` to all sprites including player before its body submission (`port/game/LevelScene.cpp:1453–1463`), whereas original player is frustum-exempt. That is a structural admission-policy difference worth logging, **not** an established visible mismatch here: the inferred 25 s player center lies inside the viewport and the recorded camera may differ from draw camera due to shake. Do not patch the culling rule based on this still.
