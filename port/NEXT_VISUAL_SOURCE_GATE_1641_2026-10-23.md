## Finding and impact boundary

The strongest **distinct, source-proven unported full-scene visual event** found in this bounded pass is the original forest-1 **fresh-save sword-pull zoom**: holding Interact during `IntroCutscene1` gradually narrows the camera's orthographic world view, then releasing it widens the view; completing the pull restores the load zoom. Linux has no intro sequence/state or dynamic zoom path and keeps the authored level zoom fixed. This can move/scale every visible forest object during the opening interaction, a larger potential footprint than a small particle, but it is **conditional on a save whose sword-pulled flag is false**. It is not established in the delivered 11-second Linux clip, and no matched Windows image or RGB severity ranking is claimed. This is a source-contract nomination, not an instruction to alter the current active-play camera unconditionally.
