## Decision

**No production visual patch for the near-black lower Forest-1 band.** In the latest delivered 11-second gameplay clip, the early Forest-1 lower band is consistent with the authored, largely black and opaque ID37 shrubbery. Do not recolor, remove, attenuate, or move XML/art on the available evidence.
