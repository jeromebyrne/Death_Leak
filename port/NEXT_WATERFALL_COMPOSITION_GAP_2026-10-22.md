# Next waterfall-top composition gap: currency-orb hover motion (2026-10-22)

## Finding and priority

The current port animates the waterfall-top currency orbs' texture frames but leaves their authored centers fixed. Windows gives each idle orb an independently phased vertical sine hover of **±20 world units** (about ±13⅓ px at the port's 2/3 scene scale), at 2 radians/second. This affects 23 repeated bright foreground accents across the traversable waterfall-top scene, including the orb groups around the falls and bridges. It is a bounded, source-proven motion/composition gap after the waterfall sheets' V scroll and the assigned splash-spray work; it is **not** splash IDs 49–51 or minification filtering. The ranking is by recurrence and visible focal placement, not a measured matched-frame pixel delta.

## Exact evidence

- Waterfall-top authors 23 `CurrencyOrb` objects (IDs 75, 77, 82, 84, 86, 88, 89, 91, 98, 100–105, 108–113, 115), each with `sine_wave_props active="true"`, `y_offset="20"`, `amplitude="2"`, randomized initial step, and `kOrb` depth. Representative ID75 has dimensions 47.6973 × 72.8312 and alpha .75 (`Game/XmlFiles/levels/waterfall_top.xml:1270-1284`); the later IDs follow in the same file through ID115. This is orb movement, not a change to the authored sprite rectangles or texture alpha.
- Windows `CurrencyOrb::Initialise` explicitly enables the sine wave, randomizes its phase, and initializes Y offset 20/speed 2 (`Game/src/GameObjects/CurrencyOrb.cpp:38-55`). `GameObject::Update` advances the wave and writes both world coordinates before rebuilding the draw transform (`Game/src/GameObjects/GameObject.cpp:187-205`). The actual formula is `Y = initialY + 20*sin(phase)` and `phase += 2*delta`; X offset is zero (`Game/src/SineWave.cpp:4-26`). `CurrencyOrb::DoIdleHover` keeps it active, whereas `DoTrackPlayer` disables it when the orb begins tracking (`Game/src/GameObjects/CurrencyOrb.cpp:210-217`). Thus permanently bobbing a collected or tracking orb would be wrong.
- The port recognizes `CurrencyOrb` and loads its animation frames (`port/game/LevelScene.cpp:428-452`), but initializes `sprite.x/y` once from XML (`:447`) and its `UpdateVisuals` loop updates scrolling positions, occlusion alpha, and animation frames without any sine position update (`:675-756`). Its draw projects the retained world Y (`:909-918`). A targeted search found no sine/bob state in `port/game/LevelScene.cpp` or `.h`. The port's current treatment therefore preserves animation and depth but omits authored hover displacement.
- Existing `port/WATERFALL_TOP_VISUAL_GAP_2026-10-22.md`, `port/WATERFALL_LIGHT_LAYER_AUDIT_2026-10-22.md`, `port/NEXT_COMPOSITION_GAP_2026-10-22.md`, and the ID49/IDs50–51 spray audits cover sheet scroll, sampling, and splash emitters, not orb hover. No claim is made that the omission dominates the broad falls or shafts.

## Bounded fix and verification

Add per-currency-orb hover origin and phase to the existing `SceneSprite`, initialized from each orb's XML sine parameters (or the source's `CurrencyOrb::Initialise` constants for this type). Advance phase by `2*dt` only while idle and in the same update zone as other `GameObject` updates, then set rendered/world Y to `originY + 20*sin(phase)`; X remains at authored X. Use a stable per-ID seed for deterministic port diagnostics, explicitly not claiming reproduction of Windows' global `rand()` history. Stop hovering when tracking starts, and preserve existing orb animation, `kOrb` painter order, alpha, collection persistence, and collision/collection semantics. Do not modify waterfall art, splash spray, water-sheet scroll, or scene filtering.

Verification: with a fixed camera and an uncollected, nontracking orb, inspect its center at controlled phases 0, π/2, π, and 3π/2 (0, +20, 0, -20 world-unit offset); confirm different IDs are not phase-locked and their animation continues. Check update-zone freeze/resume, then a tracking/collection transition so the hover ceases without a teleport or stale collision position. Compare same-camera, same-time Windows frames only after RNG phase and player proximity are controlled; Linux-only captures can establish movement but not exact pixel parity.

Read-only audit: no source/test/build/capture/GPU/display operation; this report is the only file written.
