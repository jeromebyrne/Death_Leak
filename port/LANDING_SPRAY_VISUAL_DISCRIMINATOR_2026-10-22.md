## Exact blocker and minimal next gate

The frozen production CLI in `port/linux/main.cpp:85-110` has frame/capture/save/script options but no switch to suppress *only* landing particles or produce a spray-only render. `port/game/LevelScene.cpp:1277-1282` draws footstep, takeoff and landing sprays in sequence after the player at player depth; a generic object-only filter would also suppress dynamic sprays. The source-backed contact occurs, but scene/camera/player changes between frames and dark authored layers prevent assigning any small changed pixels specifically to landing particles. Consequently a frame-locked emitter-on/off physical-GPU pair cannot be made with this frozen binary without changing the runtime or renderer. Image brightening/cropping alone cannot cure the attribution problem.

The **minimal source-backed next correction is capture instrumentation, not an art/physics retune**: add an opt-in, capture-only landing-draw suppression at the `landingSprays_` draw loop while leaving emission/update, collision, camera, player and all other draws identical.
