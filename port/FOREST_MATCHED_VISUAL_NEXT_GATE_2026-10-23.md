3. On a separately owned Linux lane, reproduce **that measured Windows history/state** with a lossless same-stage frame and state sidecar. The current port `--camera-script` is simulate-only, so do not claim a forced render match from camera X alone. Freeze/record stochastic layers or match their phases. 4. Register at least three static, noncollinear level landmarks (e.g. trunk/branch/ground junctions), report each landmark residual in native pixels, and require **maximum displacement ≤2 px** before measuring treatment. If this fails, report a camera/transform/viewport mismatch; do not interpret RGB or edge rows. In the registered frame, sample two exposed edge columns and one occluded control; record first composited edge row, a 16-pixel vertical luminance/alpha profile around it, and ID17/19/21 draw order/state. A persistent >2-px exposed-edge displacement with stable landmark registration nominates an ID17/19 geometry/UV/filter investigation. Aligned edge rows but differing profiles nominate alpha/sampler/postprocess diagnosis. Agreement at exposed edges plus no visible ID17/19 contribution at the occluded control closes **this** middle-band concern as authored composition. These are decision thresholds for the gate, not a global color-parity promise.
