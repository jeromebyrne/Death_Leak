## Earliest visible-overlap opportunity

Original `WeatherManager.cpp:101-177,254-268,314-365` creates the lightning layer before the camera-attached, looping rain spray, both at `kWeatherForeground` (20), and schedules the next flash **10.00–29.99 s after the first flash is triggered**. `ParticleSpray.cpp:215-300` advances age and respawns expired particles at their current camera attachment; therefore initial rain particles have entered the camera well before the earliest possible later flash. The original exact later-flash frame is not knowable from these files alone: its `rand()` draws share the process-global stream (`WeatherManager.cpp:245-250,260-267`). Its earliest theoretical trigger is just after first-flash time + 10 s (strict `>` comparison), with positive flash alpha on the *following* rain update. It remains within the minimum 70 s rain session.

The **current port's deterministic first rain session** is more precise: default `WeatherLightning` seed 1 (`port/game/WeatherLightning.h:10,59-63`; instantiated in `port/linux/main.cpp:293`) draws rain duration 107.48 s, then first-to-second-flash delay 24.67 s. The recurrence and frame values are source-derived only, not a game/GPU observation. Pause, weather gating, different `dt`, or different seed changes them (`port/linux/main.cpp:1066-1067`).

## Same-depth order and blend finding

Source `GameObjectManager.cpp:943-985,1021-1025,297-329` appends objects, then `std::list::sort`s by descending depth; equal-depth order is stable. Because `WeatherManager.cpp:101-177` adds lightning before rain, source draws flash **then** rain. Port `main.cpp:1132-1149` appends depth-20 flash then depth-20 rain, and `LevelScene.cpp:820-822` stable-sorts dynamic items by descending depth. The source's `Graphics.cpp:392-407` uses RGB `SRC_ALPHA, INV_SRC_ALPHA`; the port's SDL textures use `SDL_BLENDMODE_BLEND` (`port/linux/SDLRenderer.cpp:162,173-187`), with the same source-over RGB factors. `BasicLightTexture.fx:48-55` multiplies lightning texture alpha by layer alpha; `Particle.fx:47-56` multiplies rain sampled RGBA by brightness and particle alpha. Port flash tint alpha and rain tint RGB/alpha implement those respective multipliers (`WeatherLightning.h:18-29`, `ParticleSpray.h:84-99`). Thus **no specific unimplemented same-depth ordering or source-blend discrepancy is identified by this audit**. Exact D3D/SDL filtering, texture, and postprocess pixel parity, including an actual overlapping frame, remain unmeasured; this is not a Windows parity claim.
