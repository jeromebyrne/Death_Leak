## Findings

- **Saturated admission / story priority:** `PlayDevilVoice` holds the SDL device lock, removes the first `id == 0` entry from the insertion-ordered voice vector at capacity, and appends a nonzero tracked voice. The normal `Play` method still refuses ordinary SFX once the vector has 16 entries. Later ordinary SFX cannot displace a story voice. When all entries are story voices, admission exceeds 16 rather than reporting a false completion. This is deliberate and preserves the reward boundary; it is not a general unbounded ordinary-SFX pool.
- **Reward timing:** `VoiceFinished(id)` remains false while that handle is in the voice vector. The mixer advances offsets and erases only completed samples. Live `main.cpp` gates reward on `VoiceFinished(devilDialog->voice)` and updateability/manager-zone checks; the original HealthDevil likewise rewards after voice completion or deliberate SFX disable. The new admission path therefore avoids the original `id == 0` immediate-reward failure at 16 ordinary voices. Muting clears voices and intentionally completes the gate.
- **Fixture quality:** committed fixture covers unsaturated playback, 16 ordinary SFX followed by dialog, false-before/true-after completion, and deliberate mute. The current uncommitted owner edit strengthens the reward-state assertion and adds a subsequent ordinary burst; I did not edit it. The fixture does not individually prove *which* ordinary sound is evicted or exercise the all-story admission branch, but the source logic for both is direct. No independent runtime claim is made here.
- **Edge cases:** handle zero is skipped on unsigned wrap. A theoretical wrap collision with a still-active handle is not prevented, but would require billions of admissions during one still-active dialog; no reachable gameplay scenario or concrete defect established. Device-unavailable and SFX-disabled paths still return 0, matching intentional immediate completion.
