## Audit and scoped change

The existing live loop already advances gameplay, scene/player/NPC animation, shader and camera-shake state in authoritative 1/60-second steps. Input is sampled once per fixed update; audio playback and voice completion are real-time; 11-FPS gameplay film selection samples a wall clock on updates. Scripted `--fixed-dt` remains a separate one-update/one-render regression path. Opt-in `--refresh-slots` replays a sealed immutable ordered draw packet in update-free fullscreen slots, rather than interpolating mutable game state. Therefore this slice does not claim newly enabled high-Hz presentation or motion interpolation.

Two genuine timing defects were isolated. First, a long wall-clock suspension was converted to `int` before the 120-step cap, risking overflow. `FixedStepPacer` now admits at most 120 quanta before narrowing, saturates its dropped-step counter, ignores backward clock samples and preserves fractional phase. The previously rejected eight-step cap was **not** reintroduced: it had dropped startup updates. Second, the first physical Forest render can upload/compile assets after the first update and before its first present. The old pacer charged that one-time startup delay to live gameplay, creating 67 dropped quanta at 1280x720@60 and 8 at 1440x810@120 in initial 100-update runs. The live pacer now anchors wall time on the **actual first ordinary present**; subsequent updates still use the same fixed 60-Hz clock and existing 120-step overload policy. Test-only and fixed-dt paths do not rebase. No painter, shader, image, authored animation or audio source changed.
