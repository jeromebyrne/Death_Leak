## Scope / result

Read-only, non-GPU audit of the forest-1 ID19/ID17 middle-band hypothesis. **Concrete discriminator:** calculate each strip's *screen-space alpha-onset corridor* from its authored RGBA alpha, XML dimensions/position, and current render-command transform, then ask whether the questioned line moves inside that corridor with camera Y at the source depth multiplier. A line outside both predicted corridors cannot be these authored texture alpha onsets. A line inside is only consistent with them; it is not proof of composited causation or Windows parity.

Thus at **cameraY=3400 only**, screen-top of ID17 is ~464.8 and ID19 ~401.3. Any other actual camera Y shifts those bounds by `(2/3)*layerY*ΔcameraY`; do not silently assume an old capture logged its camera.
- Original sprite vertices put V=0 at world bottom (`Game/src/GameObjects/Sprite.cpp:194-235`); the port explicitly inverts ordinary `flipY` (`port/game/LevelScene.cpp:1290-1308`). Current `SDLRenderer.cpp:223-252` dispatches these unrotated repeat-X sprites through SDL texture copies, with `RepeatX.h:16-40` partitioning the X span. Horizontal flip changes the sampled X phase, **not** the vertical onset corridor. Shader `Game/ShaderFiles/BasicLightTexture.fx:11-16,53-59` samples wrapped texture RGBA and multiplies alpha. Filter/quantization can soften the exact displayed row.
- Direct read-only Pillow inspection of `Game/Media/levels/waterfall_top/ground.png` and `ground_blur.png` finds both RGBA images are 734×1024. For each source X, scan from source row 1023 upward to the first alpha meeting a threshold. `ground.png` first nonzero-alpha row spans source Y=925..963; first alpha≥128 spans 925..962. `ground_blur.png` first nonzero spans 931..966; first alpha≥128 spans 925..957. (These are ranges across all 734 columns, not a guessed sample X.) With screen top sampling source bottom and 1024 world pixels occupying 682.7 screen pixels, first-alpha offsets from quad top are respectively **40.0..65.3 px** (ID17) and **38.0..61.3 px** (ID19); alpha≥128 offsets are **40.7..65.3** and **44.0..65.3 px**. At cameraY=3400 this predicts first nonzero support roughly **y505..530 for ID17** and **y439..463 for ID19**, before texture filtering/postprocess/occlusion. Their broad, near-horizontal onsets are an intrinsic authored-asset property.

This alignment makes authored texture alpha plus distinct depth placement a specific, falsifiable explanation for the *isolated historical* pair. The prior report also shows full-scene gradients do not cleanly identify the pair. The old only-ID frames came from separate processes and lack a logged camera/effect phase, and there is no matched original-Windows frame. This audit does **not** establish that either strip is the last writer of a present full-scene line, that current HEAD has the same line, or that Linux is visually correct/incorrect versus Windows. In particular, do not subtract pre-V-fix hide images from post-fix baseline.
