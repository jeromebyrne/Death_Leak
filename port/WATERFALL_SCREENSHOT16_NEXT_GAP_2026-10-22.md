## Exact authored/draw-path cross-check

- The defining paired geometry is authored, not inferred from JPEG texture shapes: waterfall-top `Sprite` **ID32** at world `(−92.014648, 2790)`, size `674.042114×184.308380`, rotation `+0.180110` rad; **ID33** at `(803.985352, 2813)`, same size, rotation `−0.279275` rad. Both use `Media/objects/raft.png`, `kGround`, alpha 1 and `horizontalflip=true` (`Game/XmlFiles/levels/waterfall_top.xml:591-620`). The port loads these ordinary sprites from their XML position, dimensions, depth, alpha, flip and radians (`port/game/LevelScene.cpp:332-343,448-452,507-518`), projects them about `cameraX/cameraY` with the fixed 2/3 scale and submits rotated/flipped sprites (`:913-1013`). No screenshot-backed reason was found to flip, move, resize, duplicate, or bridge IDs 32/33.
- The authored camera begins at `(0,3000)`, zoom 1, follows X and **does not follow Y**, with X lag 15 and Y offset −100 (`waterfall_top.xml:3-5`). The port reads those fields (`LevelScene.cpp:145-153`) and uses camera-relative draw coordinates (`:913-1013`). Consequently a ~320-px screen translation can arise from a ~480-world-unit camera-X difference under the 2/3 scale; the latter is an **inference**, not a logged original camera position. The screenshot does not encode its original camera X, viewport/backbuffer transform, player position, or frame time.
- The broad falls (ParallaxLayer IDs **6/7/10/13/14/15/16/31**), splash IDs **49–51**, orb hover/tracking, ordinary scene filtering, shader-clock continuity and rotated fog culling have already been separately audited and implemented/reviewed (see `WATERFALL_TOP_VISUAL_GAP_2026-10-22.md`, `NEXT_COMPOSITION_GAP_2026-10-22.md`, `NEXT_WATERFALL_COMPOSITION_GAP_2026-10-22.md`, `SHADER_CLOCK_GPU_REVIEW_2026-10-22.md`, `ROTATED_FOG_CULL_GPU_REVIEW_2026-10-22.md`).
