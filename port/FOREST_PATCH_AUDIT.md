# Forest horizontal-patch source audit — 2026-10-22

**Scope:** Read-only review of HEAD `b43df4c`, original Windows C++/XML/shader/art, and `FOREST_VISUAL_COMPARISON.md`. No build or capture. The cited Steam still and port clip are unmatched camera/state/time; this audit establishes a code-path mismatch and a testable explanation, **not** pixel parity or proof that every straight edge is erroneous.

## Highest-impact remaining mismatch: fog ScrollingSprites are frozen at full authored alpha

Forest-1 XML's two very large `ScrollingSprite` fog layers are **IDs 119 and 122** (`Game/XmlFiles/levels/forest_exploration_1.xml:1630-1662`): `Media/fog.png`, each 1988×1290, `kGroundFront`, alpha **0.5/0.6**, with `effectpixelwobble` intensity **0.07** and speed **0.3**. ID 119 starts `(789.985,2954)`, moves +1000 X over 45 s, and fades in/out over 6 s each. ID 122 starts `(-125.015,2861)`, moves −750 X over 70 s, and likewise fades over 6 s. Both have `updateable=true`. These are broad softening/compositing layers, not tiny local props. At the forest-1 authored camera Y=3400 and port's 2/3 projection, their *unrotated* top bounds start roughly at screen y≈227 and y≈289, overlapping the comparison's y≈380–500 patch region; the slight XML rotation makes these only approximate bounds. The fog PNG is RGBA 1988×1290, with a transparent/low-alpha outer edge and a substantial opaque middle (read-only asset inspection), so its effective opacity matters across a wide horizontal region.

The original factory creates `ScrollingSprite` (`Game/src/GameObjects/GameObjectManager.cpp:721-724`). Its `Update` (`Game/src/GameObjects/ScrollingSprite.cpp:19-61`) increments an elapsed cycle, interpolates X/Y from the authored start/distance/time, and sets `m_alpha = mOriginalAlpha * elapsed/FadeInTime` during the initial fade; it also fades out before restarting. `DrawableObject::Initialise` captures the XML alpha in `mOriginalAlpha` (`DrawableObject.cpp:213-219`). Original `Sprite::Draw_effectPixelWobble` sends that dynamic alpha plus wobble intensity/speed to `BasicLightTexture_PixelWobble.fx` (`Sprite.cpp:673-690`); the pixel shader perturbs UVs and samples the texture with that alpha (`Game/ShaderFiles/BasicLightTexture_PixelWobble.fx:49-63`). In a fresh continuous route, approximate effective sprite alphas at 1 s are 0.083/0.100, and at 5 s are 0.417/0.500, before PNG texel alpha; exact values depend on the update instant and any level-state history.

In HEAD, `port/game/LevelScene.cpp:330-458` puts every textured non-special object, including `ScrollingSprite`, into ordinary `SceneSprite` with its starting XY and static XML alpha; it parses neither `scroll_properties` nor `effectpixelwobble`. `SceneSprite` (`LevelScene.h:11-40`) holds no scrolling cycle or pixel-wobble state. `UpdateVisuals` (`LevelScene.cpp:573-614`) updates particles, occlusion fade, and animation but never scrolling position/fade; `Draw` (`LevelScene.cpp:675-727`) sends static XY/tint to SDL, whose `DrawSprite` has foliage-sway/repeat/parallax paths but no pixel-wobble path (`port/linux/SDLRenderer.cpp:169-235`). Thus IDs 119/122 are rendered at **full XML 0.5/0.6 alpha from the first port frame**, never drift/fade, and are not UV-wobbled. This is not a duplicate of the already-implemented foreground-tree occlusion fade: XML explicitly disables `fade_when_player_occluded` on both fog sprites, and the port fade branch only handles that unrelated mechanism.

**Why prioritize:** This is a source-proven, time-varying, scene-wide compositing mismatch exactly over much of the reported patch zone. It can make authored layer boundaries/texture transitions look more fixed and pronounced in the early clip. It does **not** prove the fog is the sole or literal geometric origin of the straight edges. The XML also contains hard rectangular `ground.png`/`ground_blur.png` IDs 17/19 and broad white overlays IDs 38/39, and same-frame Linux hides in `COMPOSITION_DIAGNOSTIC_NOTES.md` show those contribute to middle strips. Those rectangles are authored; no matched original frame establishes that their edges are port-only. Earlier ID47 false upper strip, ID37 bottom band, and ID46 repeat/flip issue are separate; ID46's phase was already fixed by `b65052d`, and HEAD `b43df4c` separately corrected parallax UV/vertical mapping. Do not re-recommend either fix.
