# Forest-1 screenshot-14 composition and sprite-rotation audit — 2026-10-22

## Result

A **source-backed renderer defect** was found and narrowly corrected: the port passed authored `rotation` radians as SDL degrees, and also negated the angle. The authored forest-1 raft pieces and ID117 katana therefore appeared nearly horizontal/unrotated and nearly vertical, respectively, whereas Steam screenshot 14 visibly shows a sloping raft and a diagonal katana embedded in the branch. The corrected port uses `rotation * 180/pi`, preserving sign under the D3D Y-up to SDL screen-Y-down coordinate change. This is a renderer transform correction; no XML or art was changed.

## Attribution and correction

Original `Game/src/GameObjects/GameObject.cpp` reads XML rotation into `m_rotationAngle` and applies it with `D3DXMatrixRotationZ`, whose argument is radians (lines 349 and 205). XML ID117's rotation is `-0.630003` radians, approximately `-36.10°`; its katana texture is a narrow mostly vertical 256×256 image. Before correction, `port/game/LevelScene.cpp` passed `-object.rotation` to `draw.rotationDegrees`, so ID117 received **+0.630003°**, almost upright. The raft pieces likewise lost their authored slopes. SDL's positive degree convention is clockwise on screen Y-down, corresponding to D3D positive world-Y-up rotation after projection; therefore the sign is preserved while converting units. Corrected formula: `object.rotation * (180/pi)`.

At frame 110, the corrected image visibly restores the diagonal sword on the branch and sloping raft sections. This agrees with screenshot 14's major local geometry (branches, katana, raft and vertically arranged orbs), while camera/player/phase and atmospheric treatment differ. No further renderer mismatch is demonstrated by this unmatched comparison; the still is **not** Windows parity or Jerome's visual acceptance. The source-authored ID15 shaft and foreground assets were retained.
