## Transform-order correction to an earlier hypothesis

`port/RAFT_EXIT_PARALLAX_SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-22.md` describes the Windows world matrix as translation * scale * rotation and suggests ID15's rotated center might move with its own position. That is a misread of the *left operands* in `Game/src/GameObjects/GameObject.cpp:202-210`. Each `D3DXMatrixMultiply(out, A, B)` forms `A*B`: starting with identity, the calls form `T`, then `S*T`, then `R*S*T`. With the shader's row-vector `mul(input.Pos, World)` in `BasicLightTexture.fx:37-46`, a local center `(0,0,0,1)` reaches translation `T` unchanged by rotation/scale; local corners rotate/scale about that center. This is the centered transform model used by SDL's `SDL_RenderCopyExF` here. Thus the earlier claim that rotation pushes ID15's center away from authored `(2796.985,3207)` is **not** a reason to move this shaft or modify the ordinary-sprite renderer. This source-order correction is scoped to the transform argument; it is not a measured Windows image comparison or a resolution of every flip/UV question.
