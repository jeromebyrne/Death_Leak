# Full-frame ordered packet replay: bounded refresh-path slice (2026-10-26)

Status: **accepted as a diagnostic/packet primitive only**. A complete independent refresh-slot scheduler, sustained 120-Hz presentation and 8K output remain unproved.

## Implementation and order contract

`PresentationCommandPacket` records the renderer boundary at the existing painter call sites: BeginFrame clear; scene world zoom; ordinary sprite and sprite-batch submissions; rectangle and rectangle-batch submissions; Sepia `ApplyScenePostprocess`; and unprocessed menu/preview resolve. A sealed packet owns sprite/rectangle values and replays those commands in exactly their original order to the underlying renderer. Texture IDs are borrowed: the packet is valid only while that renderer retains the resources. This avoids re-reading LevelScene, combat, weather, menu, HUD, clocks, audio and live input on a repeated output. The recorder preserves the original batch calls, so it does not intentionally split compatible submissions.

`SaveFrame` and `EndFrame` are deliberately outside the packet. The original pre-HUD capture, final capture, film/HUD/overlay order and ordinary EndFrame remain in their original positions. The opt-in `--repeat-presentation-check` only records for diagnostic runs and, after the final ordinary EndFrame, replays the sealed final-frame commands once, captures `<capture>.repeat.bmp`, then presents. No replay occurs in ordinary runs. It is **not** a new live refresh scheduler. The test mode requires `--frames`, `--capture`, and GPU rendering (not `--simulate-only`). `make -C port presentation-command-packet-test` runs an executable fake-renderer fixture with 100,000 repeat replays, owned batch input mutation, exact command order, no additional simulation updates, and positive next-update packet replacement.

## Rejected/unresolved 120-Hz long gate and next blocker

100-update fullscreen 1440x810@120 candidate captures drifted to 1440x778 output after initialization, both with and without recorder. Three exact-base 100-update control runs retained 1440x810 and matched SHA-256 `2844f74a...`. The candidate's ordinary and repeated 1440x778 BMPs matched each other but cannot be compared pixel-for-pixel to a different-size control. A short two-update serial A/B/A did match at 1440x810. The cause of the delayed X11/SDL fullscreen output-size change has **not** been established; do not attribute it solely to packet code. Treat the 100-update 120-Hz A/B/A as rejected and preserve its evidence for display-focus/mode attribution. No sustained 120-Hz pacing, tail latency, VRAM/thermal, or 8K proof follows from the short route. The preceding 120-Hz live diagnostic p50 ~22.64 ms is also far above an 8.333-ms frame budget.

The live main loop still waits for a due fixed 60-Hz update before rendering. The next concrete step is to diagnose and hold physical output size/focus across long 120-Hz runs, then add a display-slot clock independent of `FixedStepPacer`. A refresh-only slot may replay a sealed resident packet and present it, but must not call scene `PreparePresentation`, update shader/film/audio/gameplay clocks, repeat captures, or change logical projection/input. Repeat serial physical A/B/A, positive next-update checks, measured p50/p95/p99 and capture-stage order across Forest/Waterfall/transition before accepting it.
