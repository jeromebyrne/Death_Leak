## Windows contract

- `Game/src/GameObjects/CurrencyOrb.cpp:38-73,138-207`: initialization enables the randomized sine hover (offset 20, amplitude 2), alpha .75, gravity off, X resistance 1/Y resistance 3, X max velocity 20; `native=false` selects the small type, `flame_orb`, and `kOrb`. In idle, a post-hover strict **squared distance <130²** to player position switches once to tracking and calls `AddTrailParticles()` on that trigger tick. Movement begins on the following tracking update. Tracking disables sine, aims at player collision centre, steers preferentially on the dominant axis with 2.5 normal/4.8 reversal acceleration and a 3× multiplier after >2 seconds, then rotates toward velocity (`:210-271`). The source also plays a throttled acquisition sound; that is outside this visual trail slice.
- `CurrencyOrb.cpp:326-392` and `Game/XmlFiles/materials.xml:657-660`: a small orb creates **one** attached, looping 15-particle radial spray at current orb XY and orb depth, using the material's sole particle texture `Media\\orb_shatter.png`. Source parameters: bounds 3200×1200; speed 1.5–3.4; life .1–.35 s; size 40–80; gravity .5; brightness .8–1; loop time -1; life-scale target .1; fade-in .15/fade-out .8; spawn spread 3×3. Attach offset is (0,0), with orientation tracking. Recycled particles emit from the moving orb, while already-live particles continue their trajectories (`Game/src/Particles/ParticleSpray.cpp:279-304,479-514`). Negative loop time does not auto-stop (`:319-328`).
- `CurrencyOrb.cpp:75-130,274-324`: small-orb pickup uses strict **<100²** collision distance, detaches its existing trail and makes it non-looping; surviving particles expire naturally. The 20-particle large-orb collision burst is a different path and does not apply to these forest XML orbs. Saved/load-time orb IDs are marked collected. Spawn-period orbs from smashables enter tracking by another path and should not be conflated with these authored orbs.
