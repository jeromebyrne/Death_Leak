# ID37 lower-band fidelity diagnostic (2026-10-22)

## Verdict

The lower dark band is **authored foreground shrubbery**, but the Linux port had two specific `ParallaxLayer` mapping errors. The band should not be removed or recolored. This change corrects the mapping of ID37 (and other camera-X-scrolling parallax layers); it does **not** establish pixel parity with Windows or approve the final gameplay appearance.

## Source-backed attribution

- `Game/XmlFiles/levels/forest_exploration_1.xml:612-635` defines ID37 as `ParallaxLayer`, `kMiddleForeground`, `(x=1316.985,y=2800)`, 2048×512, alpha 0.95, `shrubbery.png`, `repeatX=true`, `followcamx=true`, `followcamy=false`, X camera UV multiplier 0.000690, no horizontal or vertical flip.
- The original `ParallaxLayer::Update` (`Game/src/GameObjects/ParallaxLayer.cpp:26-35`) centers the quad on camera X. Its `Draw` (`:37-83`) overrides the base Sprite repeat-X coordinates with **one texture span**, shifted by `-cameraX * 0.000690`. In the prior port, `LevelScene::Draw` set both `uvShiftX` and `repeatX`; `SDLRenderer::DrawSprite` checks repeat-X first, so the UV shift was never drawn for ID37. The correction clears the ordinary repeat-X flag when source camera-X parallax overrides it.
- Original `Sprite::RecalculateVertices` (`Game/src/GameObjects/Sprite.cpp:19-22,219-222`) assigns V=0 to the quad's **world-bottom** edge and V=1 to its world-top. `ParallaxLayer::Draw` explicitly retains that orientation when Y parallax is disabled (`:84-100`). With the source's positive-Y-up orthographic camera (`Game/src/Core/Camera2D.cpp:12-39`), image top samples are drawn at screen bottom. SDL's usual copy places image top at screen top. The correction inverts SDL Y-flip for parallax layers relative to the authored `verticalflip` flag. This is a renderer-coordinate fix, not an art change.
- `shrubbery.png` is 2048×512 RGBA. At the center, image row 20 is opaque black `(0,0,0,255)`; row 400 is translucent black `(0,0,0,59)`; row 511 is transparent. Every pixel on row 20 is opaque. Before the correction, this produced a hard near-black *top* of the ID37 quad and a fade toward its bottom. Source mapping produces the soft shrub silhouette at the top and opaque black toward the bottom. Steam forest screenshot 14 qualitatively shows that arrangement above a black lower matte, but its unknown camera/state prevents a pixel comparison.
- The saved, pre-correction Linux hide-ID37 pair in `port/build/id-audit-2026-10-22/` changed 162,082 pixels over `[0,593,1280,720)` (as counted in `port/COMPOSITION_DIAGNOSTIC_NOTES.md`); `(640,650)` changed from `(6,5,3)` to `(112,96,68)`. This attributes the old lower band to ID37, not to ID46's leaf shadow. These images are historical attribution only; no new physical-GPU capture was run here.

## Scope and limits

The code change affects only parallax-layer drawing: ordinary repeating Sprites keep their existing whole-quad repeat behavior. It corrects ID37's V orientation and enables its camera-X UV scroll, and also applies to forest-1 parallax IDs 1/40 where applicable. It does not change XML, PNG assets, depth order, opacity, sepia, or unrelated gameplay. No Windows executable or matched screenshot-14 camera/time/viewport reference is available in this repository. Other ordinary Sprite V-orientation differences, if any, are outside this bounded ID37 investigation and should not be inferred from this case alone.
