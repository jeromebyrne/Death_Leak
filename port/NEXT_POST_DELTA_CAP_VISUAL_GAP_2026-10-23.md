# Next post-delta-cap visual gap — active HUD meter progress response (2026-10-23)

## Nomination and evidence grade

**One unowned, source-proven visible discrepancy:** after health or focus changes during active forest-1/waterfall-top gameplay, Linux draws the new meter fill immediately, whereas original Windows runs the `UIMeter` progress state machine. In particular, health loss leaves a white backing at the old fill that decays by 0.005 progress per normal HUD update, while the red fill interpolates to its new value over 0.25 seconds; focus loss snaps its cyan fill but focus gain interpolates over 0.25 seconds. This is a persistent screen-space HUD element exercised by combat and focus use/recovery, so it is a better bounded candidate than another unregistered background color judgment. **Its pixel magnitude and rank versus other remaining gaps are not measured**; this is the strongest *independent, executable source-path* gap identified in this pass, not a claim of largest RGB error.

The health meter's *outer length/cap* is already fixed at HEAD `ec5c509` and visually smoke-checked in `HEALTH_METER_CURRENT_HEAD_GPU_REVIEW_2026-10-23.md`; this report does not reopen that work. HEAD `22950f8` caps current update delta to the source 60-Hz target, but does not supply the missing HUD progress state. Waterfall foliage centre, preceding-rendered animation/Y-scroll delta, ID46, and conditional raft mips have separate fixes/owners/gates; none is relabeled here.

## Exact source-to-port evidence

- `Game/XmlFiles/UI/game_hud.xml:76-98` authors both active HUD meters. `Game/src/UI/UIGameHudScreen.cpp:36-49` calls `UIScreen::Update` and then sets health/focus targets. Its health path `:91-127` calls `SetProgress(health/maxHealth)`; focus `:129-173` calls `SetProgress(currentFocus/maxFocus)`. Source `Game/src/UI/UIMeter.cpp:4,6-16,159-170` gives the meter distinct displayed, target, previous-fill and transition-start values, retaining the previous fill when a new target arrives during an active move. In `:173-220`, normal health backing decays by **0.005 per update**; a moving bar linearly interpolates over **0.25 s** using `Timing::GetTotalTimeSeconds()`. The special `xp_meter` decrease branch `:193-199` snaps focus downward. `:48-85` draws a separate `meter_bar_middle_back.png` white backing on health loss before the colored fill; `:87-125` then draws the fixed edge/middle geometry. Draw ordering matters: the backing is not just a tint on the red bar.
- Linux `port/game/Hud.cpp:15-53` is a stateless per-Draw progress rendering path apart from the already-fixed health **length** cadence. `:38-39` computes and draws current `health/maxHealth` directly; `:47` does likewise for `focus/100`. `Hud::Load` at `:8-13` loads no white backing texture. `port/linux/main.cpp:1191-1205` draws this HUD in normal active gameplay after the scene/postprocess, with `combat.Health()`, `combat.MaxHealth()`, and `combat.Focus()`. The `Combat` default is 25 health/100 focus (`port/game/Combat.h:143`), and focus spending is live (`:89-91`), so the difference is reachable, not a dormant UI capability. No `Hud.cpp/.h` edit or HUD fixture is currently dirty/owned in the inspected tree.
- One deterministic example, after the meters have settled at full and health changes from 25 to 20: source first target registration retains full displayed red fill and old white backing; after 0.125 s of its moving state the red progress is 0.9 and the white backing still covers the prior full extent; after 0.25 s red reaches 0.8, then normal updates reduce the white backing 0.005 per update until it meets the red fill. Linux immediately draws 0.8 red progress and no white backing. These are **source-state predictions**, not observed Windows pixels. Focus 100→50 snaps on the source's next `UIMeter::Update`; a later 50→100 rises over 0.25 s, whereas Linux changes both directions immediately. Account for `UIScreen::Update` preceding the HUD's target setters when asserting exact first-frame timing.
