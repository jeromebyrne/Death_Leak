Comparing its visible curtain/readability directly with fully active screenshot 16 or dividing RGB residuals by 85/255 would conflate overlay order, color space, postprocess and quantization. The *new actionable discriminator* is therefore the packet's **pre-splash scene crop plus exact splash draw record**, not a brightness adjustment inferred from the store image.
