## Exact port mapping and insertion seam

`port/linux/main.cpp:35-42` already defines `AuthoredUI(x,y,w,h)` as `{(960+x)*2/3, (540-y-h)*2/3, w*2/3, h*2/3}`. It maps the authored top to `{0,0,1280,100/3}` and bottom to `{0,2060/3,1280,100/3}`: mathematically y=0..33⅓ and y=686⅔..720. Keep floating rectangles; raster edge treatment is backend-dependent. At 1280×720, test solid-black interior samples y=10 and 710 rather than asserting the fractional boundary pixel. Full width, white tint/alpha 255, normal top UV, **vertical flip on bottom only**. Because this PNG is a single opaque black row, visual output alone cannot prove the flip; inspect the submitted Sprite's `flipY` in CPU verification.

`port/linux/main.cpp:377` already loads `finalFrame` from `Game/Media/UI/frame_top.png` for gameplay runs. `port/linux/main.cpp:1210-12` draws the scene and postprocess; lines 1226-38 are the ordinary-gameplay branch, with the film draw at 1236-37 immediately before `hud.Draw()` at 1238. The smallest main.cpp-only patch can reuse the loaded texture and insert two sprite draws *between* those calls, guarded by `if (finalFrame)`. An owner may choose explicit `AuthoredUI(-960,490,1920,50)` and `AuthoredUI(-960,-540,1920,50)` so XML provenance remains visible, or exact equivalent logical rectangles. `port/game/Hud.cpp:40-48` loads meter/orb art; `:49-95` draws orb/text then meters and has no bar draw. Do not put the bars at the end of `Hud::Draw()`, where they could cover later widgets. No Hud.cpp/Hud.h edit is needed for the minimal fix.

**Aggregate-init trap:** `port/render/Renderer.h:46-55` orders Sprite fields as texture, source, destination, tint, **opacity**, rotationDegrees, flipX, flipY. Do not copy the nearby `main.cpp:1223` initializer `{finalFrame, {}, rect, tint, 0, true}` as a bottom-flip idiom: its `0` targets opacity and `true` targets rotationDegrees; `flipY` remains false. Use a named `Sprite bottom{...}` then set `bottom.flipY = true`, or fill all intermediate aggregate fields explicitly. The SDL path reads `sprite.flipY` for texture orientation (`port/linux/SDLRenderer.cpp:226-27`); original `Game/src/UI/UISprite.cpp:82-95` flips vertical UVs when requested. Use ordinary `DrawSprite` alpha 255, avoiding an unneeded shader/asset change.
