# Post-fractional visual discriminator — player aim-line transform (2026-10-22)

## One decision

At HEAD `2c61efb` plus the present dirty gameplay/input tree, the most certain *still-unresolved focal composition contract* I can isolate after the fractional-parallax GPU gate is the aim-line sprite's right-stick **directional transform**. The port has the 400×400-world-unit `aim_line.png` child and a firing-triggered alpha hold, but positions and vertical-flips it from movement `facing` and leaves its rotation at zero. Original Windows uses the normalized right-stick vector to select attachment side, vertical flip and angle. In opposed-stick/diagonal aiming, the visible line can therefore emerge on the wrong side and point horizontally while the body and projectile aim elsewhere. This is a source-proven transform discrepancy, not a measured Steam-vs-Linux pixel discrepancy or a claim that every 400×400 pixel is opaque.

This nomination does **not** rediscover an unreported feature. `STRAFE_AIM_LINE_SOURCE_CONTRACT_2026-10-22.md` already establishes the CPU/source contract and proposes a draw-spy gate; `CURRENT_HEAD_DIRTY_INTEGRATION_REVIEW_2026-10-22.md` independently flags the side mismatch. The distinct *post-fractional* decision here is the next **physical-GPU discriminating frame pair** on the now-bound renderer, because the existing `POST_AIM_GAMEPLAY_REVIEW_2026-10-22.md` only shows a subtle right-pointing mark during scripted horizontal fire. It never exercises opposed sticks or diagonal line rotation. Other broad apparent differences in the saved forest/waterfall stills lack matched camera/time or are already isolated: ID37 lower band, ID15 shaft, fog fade/mip, and the now physically proven fractional UV path. The measured ID37 follow-X lag is only a low-contrast transient (`FOREST_FOLLOW_LAG_GPU_DISCRIMINATOR_2026-10-22.md`), so repeating its A/B is not this priority.

## One discriminating physical-GPU experiment

After the current gameplay/input owner releases the lane, build the exact then-current source once and run **two deterministic forest-1 frames on physical Xorg/OpenGL**, fixed dt, same player XY, camera XY, body sequence, alpha-hold age, HUD and level/effect clock. Inject an eligible connected-controller state with LX left and RX right/up (e.g. left X below negative deadzone, right X/Y above deadzone), then a control with the same left movement and right stick aimed right/horizontal. Capture a lossless backbuffer/player crop and log the emitted `__player_aim_line` texture, alpha, destination center, flipY, and rotation **alongside** body facing, aim vector and fired projectile direction. Prefer an isolated aim-line draw mask in addition to the normal composite; retain the same authored art and painter slot. The diagnostic should prove an aimed diagonal has a nonzero line angle and right-side attachment consistent with the right/up aim, while the horizontal control has zero angle; merely seeing a mark is insufficient. A mirrored RX-left control distinguishes side from angle. The unmodified port predicts both diagonal and horizontal cases retain zero line rotation and movement-left attachment/flip even while the strafe body and projectile aim right. Compare the two same-camera crops for the line's support/orientation, not overall scene brightness. Log strict accelerated renderer/device and confirm the following ordinary SDL draw is unaffected by the fractional GL bind.

This is a **Linux path discriminator** and a gate for a narrowly scoped line-transform correction, not Windows pixel acceptance. If the live controller/hold route cannot be injected reproducibly, use a temporary render-only line-transform override on an otherwise identical physical-GPU frame; do not conflate changed camera targeting with line geometry. For exact original parity, obtain a lossless Windows frame/trace with controller axes, player/camera state, line alpha/angle/flip and frame history. Do not retune fog, ID37, parallax, global color or art in response to this focal test.
