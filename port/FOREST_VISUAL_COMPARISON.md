**Evidence / scope.** Compared Steam store screenshot `../store_media/screenshots/14.jpg` (original 1200×675 store JPEG) with the existing physical-Xorg GPU capture from HEAD `4d5343d`, `port/build/post-particles-video-2026-10-22/forest-1-post-particles-active-10s.mp4`, especially its `still-1s.png`, `still-5s.png`, and `still-9s.png`. Current unrelated working-tree renderer/scene edits were not in this recorded HEAD build.

**What the images support.** Screenshot 14 and the clip show the same *kind* of forest-1 scene: tall layered trunks, overhead branches, floating orbs, bridge/raft pieces, and the player. In the store still the bridge arcs at left, the large tree/branch and pickup are at right, and fine fog/grain softens depth; in the moving port capture the camera proceeds past the bridge to the large trunk. Thus the frames are **not matched camera, player state, viewport, or simulation instant**. The port preserves the major scene vocabulary but shows conspicuous straight-edged horizontal/background patches (for example around y≈380–500 in the 1 s still and y≈380–500 in the 5 s still), while the reference's depth transitions look more diffuse. This is a qualitative treatment difference, **not proof** that any particular authored layer or rectangle is wrong. The near-black bottom band is excluded from this recommendation because ID37 is being audited separately. HUD orb count and player pose also differ because the captured moments differ.

**Source-backed remaining discrepancy, independent of ID37.** Forest-1 XML ID46 is a 10,000×252 `leaves_shadow.png` Sprite (`Game/XmlFiles/levels/forest_exploration_1.xml`, around lines 765–779), `repeatX=true`, horizontal flip, alpha 0.5, foliage sway. The texture is 2048×252. Original `Game/src/GameObjects/Sprite.cpp` (`RecalculateVertices`, around lines 194–235) applies the whole-quad U span `10000/2048`, then horizontally flips the whole object via its transform (around lines 84–96). HEAD's `port/linux/SDLRenderer.cpp` repeat path (around lines 189–203) instead copies/locally flips each 2048-unit tile; `DrawFoliageSway` (around lines 286–301) also tiles locally. Since 10000 mod 2048 = 1808, the globally flipped source quad and locally flipped port tiles have different UV phase, including the partial tile. The already saved same-frame hide-ID46 diagnostic changes 52,946 pixels in y569–691 (`port/COMPOSITION_DIAGNOSTIC_NOTES.md`); that proves ID46 is visible in this forest route, **not** that it explains the broad rectangular patches or the ID37 band. The foliage-sway path exists at HEAD, so the residual bounded issue is repeat/flip phase, not missing sway.
