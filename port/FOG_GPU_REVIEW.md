# Scrolling fog physical-GPU review — 2026-10-22

## Verdict

The `299141d` ScrollingSprite XY/fade port builds and passes its focused test. On physical Xorg `:0`, accelerated `nouveau / NVE7` captures prove that forest-1 XML IDs 119/122 contribute visible, time-varying fog at 1, 5 and 6 simulation seconds. At the stationary late camera, the isolated fog's right edge advances about 14–15 screen pixels from frame 300 to 360, consistent with ID119's +1000 world-X/45 s drift, while its brightness increases as the six-second fade completes. This is a bounded Linux GPU behavior check, **not** Windows/Jerome visual acceptance or exact pixel parity. It does not establish that these sprites alone cause or eliminate the middle/background straight edge; authored ground rectangles and the unported pixel-wobble effect remain separate factors.
