## Minimal test integration requested of implementation worker

Add `port/tests/orb_trail_test.cpp` beside the existing waterfall harness (or extend that file and update its orb-only draw helper). Use its `CaptureRenderer`. For robust assertions, expose a narrow **const diagnostic snapshot** for runtime authored-orb trails keyed by persistent ID: owner key, active/attached/looping state, texture ID, depth, emission XY, and immutable copies of particle state (including `startX/Y`, `x/y`, `elapsed`, `lifetime`, `dead`). An equivalent test-only friend/accessor is fine. Do not expose/mutate live vectors, and do not rely on count of visible quads as a proxy for 15 allocated particles: alpha warm-up/culling can hide some. The capture renderer separately verifies the loaded asset path and at least one actual spray quad with that texture (unfiltered, camera centered on tested orb) plus paint depth via a targeted recorder if needed. `DEATHLEAK_RENDER_ONLY_XML_IDS=66` by itself may suppress an untagged runtime spray; clear it for trail draw checks or give the trail a stable diagnostic owner ID.

Run the **same parameterized case twice**, forest `#66` and waterfall `#75`, with separate freshly loaded scenes and fixed `dt=1/60` unless stated. For isolation, center the camera on the subject orb (other orbs may still update within 2000 units, so park the player at a carefully selected test coordinate and assert only the keyed subject trail; do not assert total global trail count). Do not call `CollectCurrencyOrbs` until the pickup phase; it is global and could collect nearby orbs. Suggested exact assertions:

1. **Precondition/save suppression.** After `Load`, keyed subject exists with authored XY, alpha ~0.75, no trail. Separately `ApplyCollected({key})` on a fresh scene: orb absent from draws, and repeated `UpdateAuthoredOrbs`/`UpdateVisuals` near its location never create a trail. Use one fresh scene for each subsequent independent boundary test.
2. **Hover/zone.** `UpdateAuthoredOrbs(0.25, farPlayer, orbCamera)` changes only Y within `originY±20`, keeps X, is deterministic in two fresh scenes, and creates no trail. With camera >2000 beyond the orb's rectangle, a positive tick freezes hover/trail. Capture the **current post-hover Y** for the radius checks; do not measure from XML origin Y.
3. **Strict acquisition and one-shot creation.** At `dt=0`, player `(orb.x+130, currentOrbY)` yields no tracking/trail. Next `dt=0`, `(orb.x+129.9, currentOrbY)` arms tracking and creates **one** keyed 15-particle spray immediately, with no XY motion on that trigger tick. A second `dt=0` or later tracking tick does not create a second spray. The first positive tracking tick may only set previous-step steering, so require displacement after **two** positive ticks, not necessarily the first.
4. **State/source constants.** On creation, assert texture ID equals renderer's `Game/Media/orb_shatter.png` load, owner key and kOrb depth 3250, `looping=true`, `loopTime<0`, fade/scale/bounds/constants above, particle count 15, each sampled speed/life/size/brightness within inclusive source ranges, signed radial directions (both signs across the fixed-seed sample), and reproducible particle snapshots for duplicate scenes following identical inputs. Test ranges/tolerances, not a Windows RNG sequence or exact hard-coded Linux random positions.
5. **Attached recycle without teleport.** Keep tracking with player **>100** from orb (or drive with a state-level accessor) and tick visuals in small `1/60` steps until at least one particle expires/recycles after the orb has moved. Assert new emission/recycle `startX/Y` is near the *current* orb XY (3×3 spread), not the original XML XY; another still-live particle must retain its advancing trajectory, not be reset to the new emission point. This requires a trail snapshot before and after a known recycle, not a brittle single-frame pixel expectation. Continue long enough to prove looping beyond 0.35 s with no duplicate spray.
6. **Strict pickup, detach, residual.** In an independent case (to avoid motion steering uncertainty), at exactly `(orb.x+100, orb.y)` `CollectCurrencyOrbs` returns 0; at `(orb.x+99.9, orb.y)` returns 1, inserts the exact key, and a repeat returns 0. The orb is hidden; its existing trail is detached and `looping=false`. Advance `UpdateVisuals(1/60, ..., camera)` for at least 0.5 s: no particle recycles, residual live-particle count never increases and reaches 0/pruned. Repeat with `ApplyCollected({key})` **on a live tracking scene** to ensure the same detach/decay; reload with that key to ensure no new trail. A new scene load/scene replacement must not retain residuals.
7. **Negative scope.** Loading a level with a native/large orb must not use this authored-small trail; a smashable-spawned `SpawnedOrb` must not increment keyed authored trail count. Forest other authored IDs remain independent. No changes to XML leaf/splash spray admission.

The 130 and 100 boundary assertions are independent: acquisition tests call only `UpdateAuthoredOrbs`; pickup tests call `CollectCurrencyOrbs` explicitly. For exact equality use matching float additions and a `dt=0` tick after hover; for inside use 129.9/99.9 (not a microscopic epsilon that rounds back to boundary). When comparing captured quads, filter by the `orb_shatter` texture ID rather than `drawn.size()`.
