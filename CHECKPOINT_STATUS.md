# Death Leak native Linux port checkpoint — 2026-10-19

**Status: partial; not a playable, performant complete game.** Branch `feature/linux-renderer-foundation`. The original Windows sources and assets remain unchanged. The backend-neutral `render::Renderer` boundary remains intact.

## Verified slices

- Original XML levels render through SDL and authored `LevelTrigger` transitions work. Deterministic `--script`, `--fixed-dt`, and `--simulate-only` controls support repeatable scenarios. Original `CurrencyOrb` pickup and limited DL2 save/resume preserve level, position, collected orbs, health, and focus.
- Original `NPCTrigger` objects spawn simplified ninja/ghost/skeleton encounters. Knife shots, enemy shots/damage, water focus consumption and drowning, and authored damage regions are connected; AI/projectile behavior is still a prototype rather than source-equivalent.
- The earlier continuation replaced the generic player movement block with `PlayerMotion`. It uses the source `Player` collision dimensions (128.959793×214.898743), 8 world units/tick max horizontal speed, 21-unit jump, 0.75 gravity amount, 0.55 vertical resistance, 0.80 idle friction, and 0.3 s edge-jump grace, scaled from the original 60 Hz target update. Landing follows authored sloped `SolidLineStrip` segments, and vertical segments stop horizontal penetration. One-way lines do not block ascent from below. This is an approximation, not a full port of `MovingSprite`/`CollisionManager`.
- Zero health now freezes active gameplay and shows original `you_died.png`. R (or scripted `T`) reloads `grass_exploration_3.xml`, matching the level hardcoded in original `XmlFiles/UI/you_died.xml`; this is a keyboard adaptation of its button action. A minimal renderer-neutral health bar and orb icon use assets and coordinates from `game_hud.xml`, but XP/count/focus text and the rest of the HUD are absent.
- This continuation adds the original five-knife burst limit, 0.5 s burst pause, 0.25 s knife lifetime, firing prohibition in water, and five-second focus recharge lockout after depletion. Idle firing now retains last facing direction. The renderer-neutral HUD also displays the original `xp_meter_bar_middle.png` as a focus meter, matching `UIGameHudScreen`'s repurposing of the XP meter. These are source-grounded rules in the existing simplified combat model, not full combat parity.
- A separate backend-neutral `audio::SoundPlayer` boundary now has an SDL2 implementation for the original three `knifeThrow*.wav` variants and an orb pickup WAV. It mixes overlapping effects and fails to silent play if no audio device is present. This is a limited SFX slice; music, positional/ambient audio, settings, and actual-speaker listening remain open.
- The level parser now preserves authored `SolidMovingSprite` collision-box dimensions and offsets even when those objects have no renderable texture. `PlayerMotion` sweeps horizontal movement against their sides, lands on their tops, and blocks ascent through their undersides. This fixes missing invisible solid-box collisions; boxes are currently static and do not inherit source object velocity. An optional wall-contact jump uses the source `Character::WallJump` constants (15-unit/tick horizontal boost, 20-unit/tick upward boost, 0.3 s lockout) and source side-contact gravity/vertical damping. The original `WallJump` method has **no live caller** in the Windows source, so exposing it via a second jump press in this port is a Linux gameplay adaptation, not established input parity.
- Water motion now uses the source `MovingSprite` 0.4 horizontal speed multiplier, four-unit/tick vertical speed cap, and 0.1 gravity multiplier. Held jump maps to the gamepad swim-up acceleration (0.3 unit/tick), allowing upward movement in authored water. Water exit, swimming/downward control, `WaterJump`, and collision-state handoff remain approximate.

## 2026-10-19 continuation: arena route and jump control

- **Root cause of the apparent ~400-unit arena climb:** the port previously gave a full 21-unit/tick impulse on a one-frame press but did not implement the original variable-height held jump. In original `Game/src/InputManager.cpp`, `kJumpInitialPercent` is 40 and each held update adds 6.5 percent until 100; `Character::Jump` and `IncreaseJump` implement this in `Game/src/GameObjects/Character.cpp`. The authored arena `SolidLineStrip` id 17 climbs from the lower approach near x≈4515/y≈-1800 to an upper ledge near y≈-1400. Invisible solid box id 18 spans x=4012..4512 and tops at y=-1424; box id 19 tops at y=-1902. These are authored static obstacles (`applygravity=false`, no animation); the port's prior static-box assumption was not the missing moving-platform mechanism here. `Character::WallJump` has no live caller in original source and is not needed for this route.
- `PlayerMotion` now starts a jump at 40% and raises upward velocity over held frames by 6.5% per 60-Hz-equivalent tick, stopping at 100%; tapping versus holding yields materially different trajectories. This is still an approximation of original update/collision ordering, not a claim of byte-identical physics.
- Deterministic source-level route: `--play Game/XmlFiles/levels/forest_exploration_1.xml --script L:1500,Q:40,L:700 --fixed-dt 0.0166667 --simulate-only` enters arena at frame 1115, ascends the authored wall/box ledge, and triggers the authored arena→`grass_exploration_1.xml` transition at frame 2008. Intermediate `L:1500,Q:40,L:100` ends at x=3756.65/y=-1271.93 grounded on the upper path. No collision bypass or inflated jump constant was added. This route does **not** prove encounter completion, survival balance, or all level routes.
- Docker build (`deathleak-linux-build:bookworm`) and `sh port/tests/integration.sh` pass. Suite now asserts arena ledge and exit transitions, and that a one-frame tap has landed by frame 100 while a 40-frame hold remains airborne. The original 1-frame-jump assertions were updated to the source-grounded variable jump behavior.
- Remaining high-impact gameplay gaps are unchanged: full collision/state manager including moving solids and water exits, source-equivalent enemies/projectiles/melee/deflection, encounter persistence, authored interactions/doors/pickups, animation, complete menus/HUD/audio/save/settings, full-game playthrough and real-display performance proof. The current port is **partial**, not a complete playable/performance-accepted release.

The parent session handles user-facing completion/blocker notification. Continue from this checkpoint; do not report the port complete.
